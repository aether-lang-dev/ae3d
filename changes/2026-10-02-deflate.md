### Compression in Aether: ae3d.deflate, gzip, ae3d.checksum

- `ae3d.deflate` compresses to zlib streams and gzip members: LZ77 over a
  32 KB window by hash chains with zlib's lazy matching, and each block
  sent with its own Huffman codes (lengths limited to 15 bits), the fixed
  codes, or stored, whichever is smallest. At level 6 it comes within 0.3%
  of zlib's size on text, image rows and zeros, and zlib's inflate reads it.
- `ae3d.inflate` reads gzip members (`inflate.gzip`), checking the CRC-32
  and length their trailer gives.
- `ae3d.checksum` holds the CRC-32, now a constant table where the PNG
  writer built one on its first call, and the Adler-32.
- The PNG writer and the scene's mesh files use them. `std.zlib` is gone
  from the engine, and the strings a compressed block used to make a round
  trip through are gone with it.
- A late joiner's horde (#501) is not deflated: measured, the quantised
  state comes down by 0.4% plain, 0.7% a byte column at a time and not at
  all as differences from the zombie before. Its positions and walk phases
  already carry as many bits as they hold.
