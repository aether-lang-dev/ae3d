### Feet that don't skate

- **A foot the clip sets down is held where it touches, whatever its
  speed** (#735, `ae3d.feet`). It was held only once slower than 0.6 m/s,
  and the box man's walk sets its foot down still moving at 1.2 m/s: its
  toe slid about 2 cm on the ground at every step before it was held. A
  foot whose ankle is within 5% of its standing height is on the ground; a
  swing never comes that low.
  - tests/test_feet: a foot set down while the body goes on at 1.2 m/s is
    held within 0.4 cm of where it touched (main: it slides on 18 cm,
    never held).
- **Untargeted channels at the node's rest** (#675, `ae3d.figure`). A
  turn or a place the playing clip does not key goes to the node's rest,
  as glTF has an animation leave what it does not target (Unity and Unreal
  do the same), another writer's move on it kept, and a change of clip
  carries it there as it carries everything else. The box man's Idle keys
  one spine bone: a walker that stopped stood in the stride it stopped in,
  its feet 12 cm up, its hips anywhere in the walk's bob.
  - tests/test_runtime_clip's bone-length check bounds each bone by where
    it was and where the cut has it: the Fox's hips go from the walk's bob
    to their rest, as the cut's do.
- **A held foot left behind steps by itself** (#675, `ae3d.feet`). A foot
  held on the ground whose clip has set it more than 8 cm away, and holds
  it still there, is lifted along an arc and set down there over 0.25 s,
  while the other foot holds the body. A walker stopping drew its held
  foot out of reach, and it hung 12 cm up.
  - tests/test_locomotion, the box man on ae3d.feet: stopped, both ankles
    stand at 0.07 m and the hips at 1.0 m (main: the ankles 0.12 m up;
    without the step, one foot 0.12 m up). Stopping, the toe slides 0.4 cm
    (main 1.8). Turning round slips 2.9% of the body's way (main 2.0, under
    5%); walking straight 0.25% (main 0.01%, under 0.5%).

### The plaza

- **The toes' skating, held or not** (#735): the way a walking walker's toe
  goes over the ground while within 25 mm of it (the box man's toe base
  stands 19.8 mm over it), held by ae3d.feet or not, in centimetres a
  walker-second, and the seconds faster than 0.2 m/s. A stance's slide
  counts held feet alone: letting go of every foot the leg could not reach
  scored 4 of 8,525 stances over 1 cm against 424 of 4,835, while the toes
  skated 60% more.
- **The car is a car on four sprung wheels** (#736): `physics.vehicle`, a
  1.2 t box on four sphere wheels on 7 Hz suspension, put at its lane's
  start rolling, steered down its lane, braked by its wheels once it has
  struck someone, and out of the world when parked. Kinematic, it stopped
  on a struck walker that had gone under it and pressed the body 6 cm into
  the ground for the seconds it stood. Seeds 1 to 8 at 20, 24 and 28
  walkers, the same engine with either car: runs with a body in the car
  past 2 s, 7 to 0; struck bodies sunk, 3.60 s to 0.62; in the car, 26.5 s
  to 16.0.

### Tests and toolchain

- **test_get_up runs a frame a fixed step** (#746). Its blend check reads
  each step's turn off the drawn pose, which a frame poses once; on a busy
  machine a frame ran two or three steps, and of six runs side by side one
  read the blend's worst turn at 14.1 degrees a step and failed (0.3 in
  the rest).
- **Aether 0.805.0** in CI: aether#2687 (a thread-local the generated C
  read, which a Windows program cannot import from the runtime's DLL) was
  fixed in 0.800.0. The 33 motion suites build with no warnings and pass
  on it.
