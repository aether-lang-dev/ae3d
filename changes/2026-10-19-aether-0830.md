### Aether 0.830.0

- **`AETHER_REF` v0.830.0.** E0902 counts a struct or a closure only when it
  escapes: a `ptr` parameter put in a struct local to the function no longer
  has to be declared `@keep`.
  - Each of ae3d's 234 `@keep` parameters was taken off and every program,
    test, tool, benchmark and the editor type-checked again. Twelve are no
    longer asked for and are gone: `geometry.inst_set_positions_yaw`'s and
    `core.model_set_instance_positions_yaw`'s `xyz` and `yaw`,
    `vkclusterlod`'s `free_pair` and `replace` (`buffer`, `memory`),
    `gltf_crowd`'s `upload_tier` (`pos`, `yaw`), and `gpu`'s `val_set` and
    `bind` (`v`). The other 222 are still E0902 without it. The generated C
    is byte-identical with and without the twelve.
  - A `ptr` given to a `string` local now warns (W1005), naming the typed
    accessor (`map.get_string`, `list.get_string`, `string.from_ptr`). ae3d
    has none: zero W1005 across the tree.
  - No quote's hash moves: `generate_shaders --check` passes on 0.830.0 with
    the branch's SPIR-V.
