### Props placed by rule (#918)

- `ae3d.scatter`, new: a district's props placed from rules. The ground is
  sampled into a height field from its models. Areas are tagged polygons with
  a wealth. Roads are polylines with a width, and exclusions are circles kept
  clear. Palettes set density, spacing, slope, height, tags, wealth,
  alignment (random, to the surface, along the road), scale and weighted
  props. `build_models` makes one instanced model per prop, so a district is
  one draw per prop. `load` reads the same rules from a JSON scatter file.
- The same seed places the same props on every machine. It uses integer
  random numbers, directions drawn by rejection, its own cosine series, and
  no fused multiply-adds. `hash` fingerprints the result.
- `test_scatter`, new: every placement is checked against its area and tags,
  the slope limit (a ramp at 37 degrees gets nothing), the road, the doorway
  kept clear and the spacing. The fingerprint is held to one value, the file
  matches the code, and every instance's matrix matches its placement.
  0 leaks.
- `bench_scatter`, new: 9,216 props over a 512 m square placed in 4 ms, after
  32 ms to sample the ground at 0.25 m.
- `docs/scatter.md`, new.
