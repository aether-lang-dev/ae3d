### A fitted figure's lean read from the figure's up (#550)

- `physics.ragdoll_tilt` compares the figure's up, as the pelvis carries
  it, with the rig's, not the pelvis body's +y with its target's. On a
  fitted ragdoll the pelvis body's +y is the hips' line to the spine, 20
  degrees off vertical on an MPFB figure, and a turn about the vertical
  swung it round a cone that read as a lean of a third of the turn: 13 of
  20 standing figures were taken for falling and let go. A figure so
  shaped, turned 0.5 rad, now reads 0 (it read 0.147); test_ragdoll_fit
  holds it under 0.02.
