### Motion: a carried load leans the walker and slows it

- `locomotion.set_load(l, kg, across, ahead)` (#509): a figure carrying
  something leans away from it, so the two keep their weight over its
  feet. The hips roll from a load at its side and pitch back from one
  ahead, by atan(load x offset / (body x the hips' height)), eased in as
  the other leans are. Its top speeds fall by half the load's share of its
  mass, to half at most.
- The body is 75 kg unless `set_body_mass` says otherwise. The hips'
  height is read on the first frame, and `locomotion.hips_height` reports
  it.
- In `test_locomotion`, a Fox carries 20 kg (its hips 0.41 m up):
  - a quarter metre to its right, it leans 0.1608 rad, exactly what is
    asked, and doesn't pitch;
  - walking, it goes 0.799 m/s, its walk slowed by 13%;
  - 0.3 m ahead, it pitches back 0.1923 rad;
  - set down, it stands straight again.

### Motion: arms that reach in time, and a showcase to watch it

- In a protective fall, the arms reach harder the further the figure has
  gone, up to 3 times their muscle 0.4 rad past the fall's start (#597).
  - Watched from the side in the new showcase, a figure shoved hard from
    behind had its arms flung back by the shove, and they came forward
    only once the body was down. Now they reach the ground before it.
  - Forwards, the protected head is the slower of the pair in 30 falls of
    30 (from 27), at 0.62 m/s on average (from 0.88). Sideways it is 30 of
    30, at 0.90 (from 0.58).
  - At full strength from the start, a figure pushed onto its heels that
    would have righted itself was pulled over by its own arms; the ramp
    keeps that. `test_falls` holds 29 every way again.
- `tools/motion_showcase.ae` (#509, not built by CI) shows the reactions
  side by side for a person, or the agent loop, to watch:
  - five drawn box men (`motion.on_figure` on the humanoid fixture) are
    shoved every 8 s: toward a wall, from the side, from in front, into a
    stagger, and into a fall;
  - each cycle prints whether each figure stands, its steps, whether it
    braced, and whether it got up;
  - `AE3D_VIEW=1` watches from the side, and `AE3D_TICK=60` with a
    snapshot burst captures it frame by frame.
