### Motion: a carried load leans the walker and slows it

- `locomotion.set_load(l, kg, across, ahead)` (#509): a figure carrying
  something leans away from it, so the two keep their weight over its
  feet. The hips roll from a load at its side and pitch back from one
  ahead, by atan(load x offset / (body x the hips' height)), eased in as
  the other leans are. Its top speeds fall by half the load's share of its
  mass, to half at most.
- The body is 75 kg unless `set_body_mass` says otherwise. The hips'
  height is read on the first frame, and `locomotion.hips_height` reports
  it.
- In `test_locomotion`, a Fox carries 20 kg (its hips 0.41 m up):
  - a quarter metre to its right, it leans 0.1608 rad, exactly what is
    asked, and doesn't pitch;
  - walking, it goes 0.799 m/s, its walk slowed by 13%;
  - 0.3 m ahead, it pitches back 0.1923 rad;
  - set down, it stands straight again.
