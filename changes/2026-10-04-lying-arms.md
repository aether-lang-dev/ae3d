### Motion: arms at rest once down, knees that give going over backwards

- Lying still, a figure's arms let go to 5% of their muscle (#606). The
  rest keep the landing's 40%. Watched up close in the showcase
  (`AE3D_WATCH=k`), a figure that had reached out behind it as it fell
  kept one arm pointing at the sky once it lay on its back. Now both rest
  on the ground.
  - Not before it is lying: let go as the hands first met the ground, the
    arms let the head down after them, and sideways falls went from 1.16
    m/s to 2.66 at the head.
- Falling straight over backwards, the knees give 1.2 rad instead of 0.45,
  only once the fall is more than 0.7 of the way backwards. A fall
  sideways turns the figure and reads partly backwards, and bent further
  there the head met the ground faster.
  - Folding the hips as well, to make it sit, was tried and dropped: it
    reached into the sideways falls too.
  - Falls, as `test_falls` measures them: backwards, the protected head
    meets the ground at 0 m/s; forwards 0.44 m/s, as it settles once the
    arms let go, under the 0.5 held; sideways 1.16. 30 of 30 each way.
- `tools/motion_showcase.ae`: `AE3D_WATCH=k` follows figure k from close
  at its right.
