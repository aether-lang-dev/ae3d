### A surface's own light: emissive colours and maps, from glTF too

- `core.model_set_emissive(m, r, g, b, strength)` gives a surface light of
  its own, linear: a lit window, a lamp's pane, a sign.
  `core.model_set_emissive_map(m, path)` says where, and its colour times
  the emissive.
  - There was no such light before: a surface glowed only all over (an
    exposure over 10, unlit), or as fire or embers. Hushlight's windows were
    a bright albedo and a point light each.
- Both renderers carry it: Vulkan's draw set has the map at binding 10,
  OpenGL's sits on unit 5. The light is added after the surface's shading
  and before the fog, so the bloom picks it up. The lit shaders were
  regenerated (`tools/generate_shaders`, glslang 16.6.0): 137 uniforms, 2,463
  offsets checked.
- glTF: `emissiveFactor`, times `KHR_materials_emissive_strength`, times
  `emissiveTexture`.
- The scene file keeps `emissive` and `emissive_path`, only when set. The
  scene DSL gains `emissive(r, g, b, strength)` and `emissive_map(path)`,
  and its writer emits them.
- Tests:
  - `test_emissive`, new: in the dark on both renderers, black, an orange
    glow, a map glowing on its white half only; OpenGL and Vulkan within 1
    of 255.
  - `test_gltf`: a fixture with factor, strength and texture.
  - `test_scene_io` and `test_scenedsl`: the round trips.
