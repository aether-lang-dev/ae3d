### The plaza's scorecard counts figures inside each other, not passing close

- **"Figures inside each other" is measured against each part's thickness.**
  The plaza's scorecard counted two figures inside each other when any of
  one's hips, chest and knees came within a fixed 0.2 m of the other's.
  Two walkers passing 0.55 to 0.6 m apart at the hips, as the crowd's
  avoidance spaces them, had a knee 0.17 to 0.19 m from the other's: that
  was counted, though the knees are 0.15 m thick between them. Now the
  test is the two parts' capsule radii, summed, from each walker's active
  ragdoll. Over seeds 1 to 4 at 20, 24 and 28 walkers on main, the count
  falls from 73.6 to 35.9 figure-seconds (with one figure struck, 39.8 to
  22.9), and the rest is what a player sees:
  - walkers' knees brushing by up to 4 cm as they pass, 9.8 s over 71
    passings;
  - figures getting up into each other, into a falling one or into a
    walker, about 15 s.

- **"In the car" is measured where the car is, on the parts' capsules.**
  The scorecard runs in the plaza's fixed step, before the physics step,
  so the bones are where the last step left them. It compared them with
  `car_x`, where the drive has just sent the car for the coming step: at
  7 m/s, 12 cm ahead of its body. A forearm resting on the bumper read
  11 cm inside. It also tested the bones' origins only, so a hand in the
  car with the elbow out read nothing. Now each bone's capsule, at five
  points along it, is measured against the car body's box at the same
  instant, and a part counts past 2 cm (`CAR_SINK`, a contact's give).
  Over the same twelve runs on main, the figure falls from 0.033 to
  0.023 s per walker struck, and what is left is real:
  - 92 strikes put a forearm or calf 2 to 11.6 cm inside the car at the
    strike, 4.5 s in all. Contacts start only 2 cm out and the car moves
    12 cm a step, so its first frame finds the limb inside
    (aephysics#167, filed with these numbers; no workaround here);
  - 20 shorter episodes after, and one figure getting up 3 cm into the
    stopped car for 2.5 s.

### A figure getting up moves out of what is in its way

- **The way up is drawn where there is room.** A figure gets up along a
  planned way, drawn exactly, with its bodies following it. Where the way
  went into another figure or the car, the bodies were held off by the
  contact (and trail the way anyway, an arm 2 to 9 cm short of its key),
  but the figure was drawn on through. In the plaza, two figures rising
  into each other were drawn up to 11 cm inside each other with their
  bodies 1 cm apart. In test_get_up, a figure lying half under a car's
  body sat up 30 cm into it, then toppled and started again.
  - `physics.ragdoll_pose_push` measures how deep a pose's capsules are in
    anything that moves (not its own bodies, not the static world), as
    contacts measure it: capsules by their segments, a hull by its contact
    manifold, and out of a hull through its upright faces. The character
    mover's planes, tried first, end once a capsule's axis is inside.
  - Each step of the way, the pose drawn and the pose a quarter second on
    are measured, and the deeper overlap moves the whole way out (every
    key, the limbs' ends, the animation it ends on) at 1 m/s at most.
    While the pose ahead is blocked, the way holds where it is, moving
    out, for a second at most: lying half under a car, the figure slides
    out, then sits up.
  - test_get_up has a fifth figure, lying half under a car-height box set
    down as low as it goes clear of it. Its drawn way stays within 1 cm of
    the box (30 cm on the way as planned), and it gets up as the others
    do, every check held.
  - In the plaza over seeds 1 to 8 at 20, 24 and 28 walkers, against main
    with the same scorecard: figures inside each other fall from 0.117 to
    0.075 s per walker struck, and 86.3% walk again (83.0%). Holding costs

    the runs' spread (legs splayed 0.035, 0.036 on main; sunk 0.0098,
