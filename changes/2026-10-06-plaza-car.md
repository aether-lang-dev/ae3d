### Plaza: the car is a body, and struck walkers stop turning their heads

- **The car strikes with its own body.** The plaza's car is now a kinematic
  body with a box collider. Before, it was only drawn: a walker it reached
  was thrown by an impulse at the hips, and the car went through the bodies
  it had struck down. Now a walker in its lane is handed to its active
  ragdoll just before the bumper reaches it, and the car meets its bodies.
- **The blow is felt where the car met it.** The car reports each body it
  strikes (hit events). `motion.hit` takes a new `push` argument, which
  defaults to true. With `push` false, the walker feels the blow on that
  bone, losing muscle and reacting with its arms, without a second push on
  top of the contact's.
- **A struck walker's head is the ragdoll's.** Its look at the car is let
  go when it is struck. Left on, the look's turn went into the joint
  targets the ragdoll followed, from a head the ragdoll had already
  turned, and a risen walker's head turned on and on. With 20 walkers over
  60 s, risen walkers' heads turned faster than 2 rad/s for 19.7 s in
  total; now 5.7 s, the rest their stagger's own. The plaza reports this
  as "heads spinning".
