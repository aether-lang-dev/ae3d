### Motion: clip changes without a pop, feet that plant, a player's figure, staggering and writhing

- `figure.play_blended` inertializes a change of clip (#574): each node
  keeps its offset from where it was drawn and the speed it had, decaying
  along a quintic over the blend. Walk to Run on the Fox steps a bone
  6.7 units and 12.5 degrees in a frame at most, inside the clips' own
  8.4 and 25; a cut is 59 and 89. Each quintic keeps its own rest time:
  one that rested early was evaluated past it and ran away, and a stop
  from a run spun an arm 175 degrees in a frame. `layer_play` plays a
  clip over the bones under one bone, or adds its change from its first
  frame (`additive`).
- The crowd plants its feet (#575): the body moves by the root's travel
  over exactly the stretch of the walk each step plays, a speed limit
  slows the walk with the body (held to 60% it slid by 40%), standing
  stops the walk, and the heading turns at no more than 3.5 rad/s. A clip
  made in place takes its travel from the foot on the ground, along the
  way the feet go back: counted by length, a foot still landing added to
  the walk and made the Fox's 24% long.
- `ae3d.feet` puts a figure's feet on the ground under them (#577): two-bone
  IK onto each foot's ground, the pelvis eased down at no more than
  0.6 m/s for the lower foot, a foot on a slope turned to lie on it, a
  planted foot locked where it was set (2 mm while the body moves 6 cm).
- `ae3d.locomotion` is a player's figure on a character controller (#576):
  momentum, the walk or run played at the body's speed over the clip's
  own, inertialized changes, the facing capped at 4 rad/s, the hips leaned
  into turns and changes of speed, a step climbed by the drawn body at
  1.2 m/s. On the Fox the foot on the ground drifts 0.19% of the body's
  way walking, 1.7% at half the stick, 0.14% running.
- A shove one step does not catch, a figure staggers from (#414,
  `staggering`): quicker catch steps in a run while the capture point is
  within three strides, the protective fall held back to 29 degrees of
  lean meanwhile. The first step is tried out to two strides (at one and a
  half it waited until the figure was going over). From behind it now
  stands to 360 N·s, against 270 without stepping. Stepping measures its
  margin from the ankles, 24 cm, and aims the thigh within the hip's cone.
- A `POWERED` figure down and hurt writhes (#414, `set_writhing`,
  `writhing`, `wound`): the legs draw up and let down, the arms fold over
  its front toward where it was struck, fading over the second half of its
  time. Caught on its hands and knees it lets go and goes down first; face
  down it turns over, pushing with the arm on the higher side, the leg on
  that side drawn up and across and the trunk twisted the way it rolls.
  `tests/test_writhe.ae`: the hip swings 0.51 rad, the arm folds to a
  cosine of 0.60 against 0.01 at rest, 8 cm of drift, stopped at 3 s.
- `tests/test_stagger.ae` and `tests/test_writhe.ae` exit 1 on a failure;
  they printed it and exited 0.
