### Motion: every writer of the hips and legs takes its own move off; the plaza's scorecard reads knees and hips as a body has them

- **A walker standing in its Idle keeps its hips and feet where they
  belong (#673).** The figure, `ae3d.locomotion` and `ae3d.feet` all move the
  box man's hips each frame. Its Idle does not key the hips' place, so
  nothing put them back between frames. Each writer now takes its own last
  move off before it makes the next:
  - the figure, on a node another writer has moved since it drew it,
    takes off only what it added over the clip last frame, its transition
    offsets and layers. Its base is its own pose, never the other's move,
    and a change's offset leaves out what a writer has moved the node by
    since the figure drew it;
  - `figure.put_back(f, node)` and `figure.turn_put_back(f, node)` say
    whether the figure put the node's place and turn back this frame, so a
    writer knows whether its last move is still there;
  - `ae3d.locomotion` (lift, lean and foot hold on the hips) and
    `ae3d.feet` (told the figure with `feet.set_figure`: the pelvis drop,
    and the legs' solved turns) take their move off by how far it moved
    the node, not only where it still held exactly what they wrote. A turn
    comes off on the node's side, the figure's being on the other.

  Before, each writer's move went on top of the others' frame after
  frame. Standing walkers had their hips 28 cm up with their legs folded
  under them, or their feet 13 cm in the ground. With the place fixed and
  not the turn, the box man posed by motion matching turned over 168
  degrees, its head under its hips: its walk keys the hips' place, not
  their turn.
  - `tests/test_blend_writers.ae`: under a lift that differs a little
    each frame, through Walk to Run to Idle, the hips do exactly what they
    do with no lift, to 1e-4 m (before: 5.6 cm steps, 21.5 cm off).
  - `tests/test_hips_writers.ae`: the box man on a 10-degree slope across
    its way, stepping on and stopping every second for 12 s, stands after
    every stop with its hips, less the pelvis drop, where they stood after
    the last, to the micron, and no foot under the slope. Without the
    writers' fix: the hips stood 0.23 to 0.30 m over the floor, not 0.99,
    and a foot went 95 cm under the slope.
  - Untargeted channels at the node's rest, as glTF has them, made the
    stance right but slid the trailing foot 7.8 cm on a stop: left for a
    stop that steps (#675).
- **A bake leaves the rig as it found it.** `gltf.bake_bank` put every
  node on the clip's last sample, where a clip played next that does not
  key a node left it; it now puts them back. `locomotion.attach`'s own
  measuring does the same and is left for #676: put back, the feet read
  their true standing heights, and a turning step's skimming foot, held
  before by a threshold 1.7 cm too lenient, slips 6.1%.
- **The plaza's scorecard reads a knee against its own hinge.** A
  knocked-down walker's thigh flung out to the side turned its knee's
  hinge with it, and a knee bent the right way read as bent backwards
  about the line across the hips. The hinge is now that line as the walker
  stood, carried by the thigh. A new line, legs splayed, counts a thigh
  half again past a person's reach (ahead 125 degrees, back 25, out 45,
  across 30). Most of what it counts is aephysics#129: the human's hip
  cone lets a limp thigh fall out 98 degrees, where a person's goes 45.
- **Plaza walkers start on the floor.** A walker put down inside the deck
  stood in it with its feet on the deck's top, 0.36 m over its floor, and
  its legs folded, for the 3 s it took to walk out. Walkers are now put
  down clear of the deck, the steps, the ramp and the kerb.
