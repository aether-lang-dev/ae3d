### The Vulkan rays and device crowds in Aether

- The scene's acceleration structures move from `native/gpu/vulkan.c` to
  `ae3d.vkrays` (#402), on `contrib.vulkan.vk`'s acceleration-structure
  commands: a mesh's bottom-level structure built into the upload batch
  beside its copies, the frame's top-level structure built once a frame in
  a ring of two from the instances the renderer adds (the static ones
  reserved and kept, the rest written each frame), the shadow rays'
  budget, reach and figure count, and a crowd's pose structures -- its far
  mesh skinned on the CPU at every frame of its pose bank, in the same
  floating-point order as the C, a structure each, their addresses in the
  buffer the crowd's sort reads.
- The crowd sorted on the device moves to `ae3d.vkcrowd`: the figures'
  state written into the frame's host-visible buffer over every core
  (`ae3d.jobs`), the compute pass that sorts them into the near, far and
  picture tiers and counts each into an indirect draw, the barriers around
  it, and the tier draws through the backend's one remaining indirect draw
  (`ae3d_vk_draw_indirect`).
- The SPIR-V comes from Aether: `tools/generate_shaders.ae` writes
  `ae3d.vkspirv` beside `vulkan_shaders.h`, the same words, and the crowd's
  sort, the overlay and the rest are made from it. Run on this tree, the
  generator writes the module byte for byte as it is checked in.
- `ae3d.vkhost` makes a buffer in memory of any kind and uploads what is
  made once outside the frame; `ae3d.crowd` and the renderer call the two
  modules instead of the C.
- Measured on an RTX 4070 Ti, `examples/zombie_city` (400 figures sorted on
  the device and in the rays, 1280x720), the C then the Aether, back to
  back, three rounds each: the same 247 draws, 221,796 triangles, 4
  program and 177 material binds; the GPU passes the same (shadow 0.135 ms
  both, scene 5.103-5.262 ms against 5.092-5.274 ms, post 0.115-0.116 ms
  against 0.116-0.117 ms); the engine's CPU work a frame 1.53-1.62 ms
  against 0.86-1.07 ms (median 1.546 against 0.918). With the rays off
  (`AE3D_RAYS=0`, 425 draws, two rounds each) the two are within
  0.05 ms (0.769-0.809 against 0.715-0.760): what went down is the rays'
  part of the frame.
  `test_backend_parity` gives the same numbers channel for channel (21
  comparisons); the 55 suites that draw on Vulkan pass (`test_dlss` skips
  without the SDK, as before); the Khronos layer with synchronization
  validation reports nothing on fourteen of them, the ray, crowd and
  overlay suites among them (`test_engine_order`'s five layout errors are
  the C's as well).
- `vulkan.c`: 6,490 lines before, 5,154 after.
