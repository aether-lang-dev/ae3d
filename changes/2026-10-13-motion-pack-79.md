### A figure with ankles catches itself as one without does

- **The legs pass each other's feet** (`ae3d.physics`, `ragdoll_cross_legs`,
  #709). The legs pass each other below the thighs (#481) through filter
  pairs of the shins and of each thigh with the other shin. With ankles,
  each foot is a body of its own, not a shape on its shin, and no pair
  held it. A crossing foot met the other leg's shin and was stopped from
  3.1 m/s to 0.3 in a step, 4 cm over the ground; the figure stood on the
  other leg and fell. Now an ankled figure passes each foot by the other
  foot and the other leg's shin and thigh as well.
- **An ankled figure weighs what its figure does** (`ae3d.physics`,
  `ragdoll_bone_share`). Each calf carried its shank's and its foot's share
  of the body, and each foot none, so it kept its solid capsule's 1.8 kg:
  3.6 kg too heavy, all at the ends of the legs. Now the calf takes the
  shank's share (4.33%) and the foot its own (1.37%).
- The box man POWERED, shoved at the chest from 16 ways round it, 4 s each
  (`build/scratch/shove_sweep.ae`):

  | | 1.0 m/s fell | 1.5 m/s fell | landing soles off level, 1.5 m/s |
  |---|---|---|---|
  | without ankles | 0 of 16 | 1 of 16 | 4.7 cm |
  | ankles, before | 6 of 16 | 13 of 16 | 8.3 cm |
  | ankles, now | 1 of 16 | 2 of 16 | 5.6 cm |

  Without either fix the ankled figure still falls more than three times
  in 16. tests/test_ankled_steps holds both: the masses, and the falls to
  twice the plain figure's plus one (2 against 1; 12 without the pairs).
  Ankles stay off by default until the plaza and the showcase say so.
