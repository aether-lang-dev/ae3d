### Aether 0.829.0

- **`AETHER_REF` v0.829.0.** A quote names an array of a distinct type by
  its element (aether#2972): a captured struct's `vec4[4]` field is
  `vec4[4]`, not `UNKNOWN[4]`. The composite's haze is written on it as one
  captured struct.
  - A string given to a `byte[]` or other slice parameter is now E0200
    (#2971). No ae3d call does that: every module, program, test, tool,
    benchmark and script type-checks on 0.829.0 unchanged.
  - A literal reaching a `ptr` return or parameter is its characters
    (#2967 and its sibling). The unused-static, -local and -helper fixes of
    0.825.0 to 0.829.0, and the four ownership fixes, leave ae3d no
    workaround to drop.
  - No quote's hash moves: `generate_shaders --check` passes on 0.829.0
    with main's SPIR-V, 53 shaders, with glslang 16.6.0.
