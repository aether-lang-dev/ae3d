### A figure handed back to its animation blends into it, no jump at the end

- **The blend toward the animation as it is now** (`ae3d.motion`). A
  POWERED figure handed back to its animation (`set_mode(ANIMATED)`) is
  drawn from where its bodies stand into the animation over BLEND (0.25 s).
  It blended toward the animation as it was when the blend began, and as the
  blend ended the figure was let go to the animation as it was by then: it
  jumped the way the animation had gone meanwhile. Siltreach's fighters,
  handed back into their guard after a get-up, turned the drawn pelvis 7.6
  rad/s on average and 21 at the most, every time at the blend's end (the
  neck 17 to 30). Now the blend takes the animation each pose phase, after
  the clips, as they have just posed it.
- **A pose shown is kept as written** (`ae3d.physics`,
  `ragdoll_show_pose`). The rig drawn from a pose was not kept as written by
  the bodies' side, as the bodies drawn onto it are, so the next
  `capture_animation` took the pose shown for the clip's wherever the clip
  keys nothing. Blending toward the animation as it is, a walk's neck, which
  the walk does not key, held still through the blend and was let go 1.1
  rad in a frame.

    brings its forearm into the wave, never faster than 15 rad/s (9.2; on
    main it held still through the blend and turned 85 rad/s in a frame as
