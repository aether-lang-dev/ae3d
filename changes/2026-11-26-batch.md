### Added

- `ae3d.batch`: a frame described in one buffer by a host that is not
  Aether (a sae page running three.js, through its `AeRenderer`), drawn into
  a canvas and read back. The buffer carries the eye's view and lens, each
  draw's world matrix and material, and the meshes that are new or changed
  since; meshes are kept between batches under the host's ids. It is
  checked whole before anything changes, and one that is malformed or over
  the host's limits (`batch_set_limits`: meshes, vertices, draws) is
  refused with its reason. OpenGL only so far. The layout is in the
  module's header.
- `ae3d.batchmaterials`: three.js's MeshNormalMaterial as a quoted vertex
  and pixel shader pair, the normal in the eye's space packed as three.js
  packs it.
- `gl.renderer_canvas_pixels` (a canvas read back, rows top first) and
  `gl.renderer_draw_canvases` (the canvases drawn and nothing else), for a
  host with no frame of ae3d's around them.
- `tests/test_batch`: a cube's faces, at their centres, the colour of their
  normals in the eye's space to 2/255, worked out from the pose and not by a
  renderer; a second pose moves the silhouette; five malformed or
  over-limit batches each refused with nothing changed. With
  `AE3D_BATCH_FILES` it draws a page's own batches: three.js's README cube,
  its batches made on sae's engine, held by three.js's own maths to within
  1/255 on Mesa's llvmpipe.
