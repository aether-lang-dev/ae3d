### Stances and cover: crouch, prone, crawl, cover, peek (#988)

- **Stances as a graph parameter** (`ae3d.stance`, `stance.stances(c, takes)`):
  `stance_set(s, STAND | CROUCH | PRONE)`, the graph's `"stance"` beside the
  mover's parameters. The crouch idle and the forward crouch walk are
  Universal Animation Library's (`Crouch_Idle_Loop`, `Crouch_Fwd_Loop`,
  retargeted); the ways it has no take for (back, either side) are the
  stand's gaits crouched (`stance.crouched`): the pelvis lowered and the
  trunk leant by as much as UAL's crouch is lower and leant than the stand
  (40.6 cm, 33.6 degrees), the legs bent by IK to the take's own feet. No
  prone or crawl take is at hand: both are made from the stand, the body
  laid on its front, the head raised, the forearms on the ground, the toes
  tucked; the crawl's toes and hands planted in turn at 0.3 m/s.
- **Changes that keep the feet:** stand and crouch cross-fade with both feet
  planted by leg IK where they are drawn (the feet's own IK off, eased back
  in after); crouch to prone and back by a take made from the figure as
  drawn when the change begins, the hips going ahead onto the knees then
  down, kept within the legs' reach, each ball of the foot held as the foot
  rolls onto its toes. Feet: 0.0004 mm (stand to crouch), 1.4 mm (back),
  0.54 mm (to prone), 0.41 mm (back); the fastest joint 61 to 81% of the
  takes' own plus the change's easing.
- **The capsule follows the stance** (`physics.character_set_shape`): 1.69,
  1.06 and 0.65 m; raised only where there is room
  (`physics.character_room`): under a 1.45 m ceiling, asked to stand, it
  waits crouched and stands once out from under it.
- **Cover** (`cover_probe`, or `cover_take` with the game's data: a face's
  two ends, its height, the way out): high cover standing, low crouched,
  facing out; the back laid on the wall by the skin (`cover_lean`), 4.2 cm
  off it at worst side-stepping at 0.6 m/s; round corners found by probe;
  a peek out past the end on the target's side (or up over low cover) aims
  the trunk within 0.000001 degrees.
- Crouch-walking at the take's 0.652 m/s: 0.44 mm of toe skate; crawling at
  0.3 m/s: a ball of the foot slides 1.1 mm at worst while down.
- **Animator:** a cross-fade into a state a matching transition chose enters
  it where the match chose and spends the choice (`crossfade_to`): left set,
  a later `play` of the state entered it at the stale time.
  `transition_count` and `transition_from` let a layer of states condition
  the transitions before it.
