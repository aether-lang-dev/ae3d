### A figure shoved into a wall it can reach catches it

- **Held by the wall, not falling** (#590, `ae3d.motion`). A figure shoved
  hard toward a wall braces on it, but going over past the stagger's tilt
  it fell all the same: the protective fall took the arms and reached them
  for the ground, the wall took its chest, and it lay against the wall 64
  degrees over (the motion showcase's bracing figure, shoved at 2.4 m/s
  from behind, at walls 0.75 to 1.05 m straight ahead). Now a figure going
  over toward a wall it braces on, within an arm's reach of the braced
  shoulder (WALL_REACH, 0.65 m, along the way it goes), or already against
  one (a body above its feet touching the static world where it stands
  upright), is held by the wall up to WALL_MOST (0.95 rad): its hands take
  the wall and it pushes back upright.
  - Over 65 shoves at 2.4 m/s (walls 0.6 to 2.4 m off, 60 degrees either
    way of straight ahead), 31 go down against 34, and every fall starts
    where it did (0.70 to 0.85 rad). With the reach at 0.75 m, 30 went
    down, but falls toward walls out of the hands' reach started as late as
    1.2 rad; taken from the chest, as late as 1.2 rad too. The walls it
    catches it leans on 0.73 to 0.82 rad at the most: held to 1.2, the
    showcase's bracing figure, slipping down its wall, went over from
    there, late; held to 0.85, a fall began at 0.85 rather than 0.72. Held
    by any body it touches (a car, another figure), not only a wall, a
    struck figure pressed against a car would not have fallen.
  - tests/test_wall_catch: the box man shoved at 2.4 m/s at a wall 0.75 and
