### Animation over the network (#911)

- **A character's inputs and events, not its poses** (`ae3d.netanim`).
  Every peer runs the same graph from the same inputs: the velocity and
  facing asked, the look and the aim, quantised before the owner's own
  character takes them, and its events (a blow, a knockdown, a get-up, an
  act) stamped with their tick. From the same input stream the client's
  mannequin is the host's to the bit at every tick of a walk, a run, a turn,
  a stop, a knockdown and its get-up (test_net_anim), and that trail of
  hashes joins the determinism trail CI compares across its three runners.
- **Streams** (`net.send_stream`, protocol 9): a game's blocks of bytes sent
  unreliably, each carrying what a lost one did, counted as
  `TRAFFIC_STREAMS`. A figure's block every two ticks carries its last
  twelve ticks of inputs as runs and every event of the last half second.
  Under 150 ms of latency, 30 ms of jitter and 5% loss the client's feet and
  hands are the host's at every tick over three seeds; a walking figure
  streams 538 bytes a second.
- **Drift found and eased out**: a compact state twice a second (the
  object's place, the mover's velocity and facing, the graph's state and
  time) compared on each peer; parted, the place and facing eased back
  (never over 0.5 m/s), the springs' velocity taken (`mover.correct`) and
  the graph played at the owner's state, inertialized. A downed figure's
  ragdoll streams as physics state ten times a second and is compared.
- **The same sin, cos, atan2, acos and exp on every platform**
  (`ae3d.fdlibm`, Sun's fdlibm in plain arithmetic, within an ulp of each
  libm: test_fdlibm). The mannequin's trail parted on all three CI runners
  at its first tick: glibc's, Apple's and MinGW's libm round differently in
  the last bit. core's sin_of, cos_of, atan2_of, acos_of, exp_of and its
  quaternions, and the libm calls of physics, motion, mover, ikrig, lookat,
  matching, avoid, idle, items and netanim now go through it; with every
  libm function moved an ulp (a link-time shim), the trail no longer moves.
- `net.hub_set_seed`: another run of the same conditions on the loopback
  hub, to sweep.
