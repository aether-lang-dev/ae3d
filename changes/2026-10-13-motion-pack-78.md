### A figure on its clip carries the parts on their own muscles

- **The pelvis holds the clip's turn while it carries bones off their
  anchors** (`ae3d.physics`, `carry_pelvis`, #772). On an ANIMATED figure
  with its spine and head on their own muscles (set_part_mode POWERED, as
  a game has them after a blow, #658), only the pelvis's own anchor held
  the trunk's turn: a 5 Hz spring scaled to the pelvis's inertia, the
  legs' anchors carrying the pelvis only by the hips' points. With the
  trunk leant 20 degrees after the clips (Siltreach's slip), its weight
  turned the pelvis body 12 degrees off the clip. The own bones are drawn
  from their joints on the clip's pelvis, so the bodies stood 13 cm from
  the drawn trunk at the neck, and blows aimed at what was drawn met the
  bodies elsewhere. Now, while any bone is off its anchor (on its own
  muscles or limp), the pelvis's anchor turns it at 30 Hz: 1.4 degrees,
  the neck body within 1.6 cm of the drawn neck, at 20 or 40 degrees.
  A pose written after the clips needs no hook of its own: the bodies'
  anchors take the rig as each fixed step begins.
  - tests/test_part_powered: the powered spine leant 20 degrees after the
    clip keeps the pelvis body within 3 degrees of the clip's and the neck
    body with the drawn neck to 3 cm (2.4 degrees, 1.1 cm; 11.8 and 12.9
    without the fix). The jab's snap is as it was (3.4 cm).
- **The plaza scorecard counts figures inside each other past a contact's
  give** (`tools/plaza_showcase`, OVERLAP_GIVE 2 cm). Two capsules touching
  are a centimetre or so into each other through the solver's slop: of the
  frames counted as struck figures inside each other, 137 of 245 were
  under 2 cm, and 104 of 240 between walkers.
- **The gpu suites pass on CI and free what they build** (`ae3d.gpu`,
  #774). test_gpu and test_gpu_fxaa ended on "N/N checks passed", not the
  "all checks passed" line ci.sh passes a suite on, so they failed every
  leg that ran them. On macOS test_gpu also leaked its tree: ae3d.gpu had
  no way to free a shader or an image. Now `gpu.shader_free` frees the
  tree once a node, since a tree holds a node in several places;
  `gpu.image_free` frees an image; and `shader_body` frees the empty block
  it replaces. A failing leak check prints each lost root's whole stack.
- **The plaza measures the struck walkers' heads and the bodies walked
  through every step** (`tools/plaza_showcase`). They were measured only
  while the car crossed, so they went uncounted while most of the struck
  got up and walked off, and a head's turn was taken over the whole wait
  on the next crossing's first step: 18 to 42 rad/s, with the neck's
  bodies turning at 0.07. Over seeds 1 to 4 at 20, 24 and 28 walkers,
  heads spinning reads 6.3 s (5.2 before) and walked through 3.0 s (0.9);
  the rest of the scorecard is unchanged.
