### Motion: a figure stepping back bends its trunk forward over its hips

- **Shoved from in front, a `POWERED` figure steps back with its trunk bent
  forward over its hips, as a person's is (#642).** While a stagger goes
  mostly back (its backward speed over half its speed), the balance aims
  the chest 0.2 rad forward for each m/s the centre of mass goes back, the
  lower spine half that, at most 0.2 rad: the hip strategy. Upright over
  its steps, the body's mass stayed over its heels and each step back
  landed short of a capture point still running away.
  - The box man shoved from in front at 1.0 to 1.6 m/s, at seven angles
    from -30 to 30 degrees, stands out of 17 of the 28 shoves, against 12;
    from the side and from behind, 55 of 56 as before. The bend's size was
    swept: 0.1 rad per m/s stood 14, 0.4 stood 15, 0.5 stood 12.
  - Bent for any backward part of a stagger, 0.6 rad per m/s, a side or a
    forward stagger that turned a little was bent too, and the box man
    stood out of fewer shoves every way (58 of 84, against 67).
  - The bend is let go once the stagger no longer goes back, when stepping
    is switched off, and the moment the figure falls: the balance stops
    with the fall, and would have left the bend on through it.
  - `tests/test_balance.ae`: at 1.0 m/s from in front the chest goes 3 cm
    or more ahead of the pelvis on average as it steps back (4.5 cm;
    upright, 1.1); it stands out of all seven shoves at 1.0 and 15 or more
    of the 21 from 1.0 to 1.4 (17; upright, 6 and 12); and no figure that
    falls is left bent (without the let-go on falling, 3 were).
