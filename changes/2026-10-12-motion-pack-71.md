### A stagger's feet put back under the hips

- **The settling step after a stagger** (#697, `ae3d.motion`). A person
  shoved off their stance steps back under their hips once the stagger is
  over. A figure stood on whatever stance its last catch step left, narrow
  or split, and the next shove felled it. Now, once it has stood still for
  0.5 s (slower than 0.12 m/s, leaning under 0.15 rad), the foot more than
  10 cm off its home place that bears the least steps back there. Its home
  is the stance it stood in before it last stepped. The body leans off the
  foot first, as a step aside does (#705, `lean_onto`), for 0.2 s at the
  most; a foot still loaded then is not lifted, and the step is called off
  and tried a second later (HOME_CALM, HOME_SPEED, HOME_TILT, HOME_OFF,
  HOME_SHIFT, HOME_RETRY).
  - tests/test_settle: 60 staggers on the IK rig (shoved from its side, in
    front and behind, 1.0 to 1.6 m/s, 0, 10 and 20 degrees either way),
    left to rest, then shoved 1.2 m/s from the side. At most 4 of those at
    rest stand more than 3 cm off their stance (1 now, 18 on main), and the
    second shove fells at most 3 (none now, 6 on main).
  - Over 180 staggers (the same 60 three times, turned 3 degrees and 0.05
    m/s either way): stances 0.5 cm off home on average (8.4 cm), the
    second shove fells 4 (19), and first shoves fall in 8 as before.
  - A tenth of the settles lean the pelvis 0.18 rad as the weight goes
    over (0.03 without them): the lean tips the body over the stance foot
    more than it carries the hips across. Leaned off for 0.4 s, and lifted
    loaded when not unloaded by then, 15 fell from the first shove; at 0.2
    or 0.3 m/s for longer, the lean tipped the body as far and seldom
    finished.

### Heading after a hard side shove (#752)

Settled back under its hips, a figure shoved hard from the side also ends
facing closer to the way it faced. test_stagger's side shoves, each moved
up to 0.05 m/s either way, over 30 sets, the worst turn a set:

### A step ended mid weight shift lets the stance leg go (#756)

- **`end_step` lets go of the stance leg too when a weight shift is under
  way** (`ae3d.motion`). A shift aims the stance leg as well as the
  swinging one (`shift` before a step back, `lean_onto` before a step
  aside); its stance aims were cleared only when the shift completed. A
  step ended while its shift was under way, as one is the moment a figure
  starts to fall, left the stance leg held where the shift put it through
  the fall and while it lay. After pack 70 it showed in the plaza as legs
  splayed, 0.053 s a struck walker against main's 0.033: over six of the
  worst runs, 15.5 s while falling and 8.2 s lying (main 4.6 and 2.4), now
  0.32 and 0.2. The step back's shift leaked the same way on main.
  - tests/test_stagger: a step ended while its lean is under way (shoved
    from its left at 1.1 to 1.6 m/s, stepping turned off within the lean)
    lets both legs go; with the stance leg left held, 7 of 7 cases fail.
