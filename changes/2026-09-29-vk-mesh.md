### The Vulkan renderer's meshes uploaded from Aether

- Uploading a mesh moves from `native/gpu/vulkan.c` to `ae3d.vkmesh`
  (#402), on `contrib.vulkan.vk`. The module makes the vertex, index and
  skin buffers in device memory (`ae3d.vkhost`), stages their bytes in
  sixteen-megabyte chunks and records the copies into the backend's upload
  batch, which still runs once before the next frame rather than once a
  buffer. The backend adopts the buffers into the table its draws read
  (`ae3d_vk_mesh_adopt`) and builds the mesh's structure for the rays into
  the same batch.
- Identical geometry is still uploaded once and shared: the module keeps
  the bytes each mesh was built from, compares them exactly, counts the
  models drawing each and frees a mesh with the last of them. A mesh
  edited after it was registered goes up on its own, and the models still
  drawing the old shape keep it. A skinned mesh keeps no copy now, since
  it never shares.
- `tests/test_vk_mesh` checks it on the device: three cubes built apart are
  one upload and two shares, an edited one goes up under a handle of its
  own while the other two keep theirs, the table's slots fall as the last
  model lets go, and the frame matches OpenGL's (282 of 147,456 channels
  apart, the anti-aliased edges).
- Measured on an RTX 4070 Ti, the C then the Aether, twelve runs each,
  back to back: a one-million-triangle sphere is added in 7.5 ms against
  8.0 ms (median) and added and drawn in 12.6 ms against 12.4 ms; the
  zombie street's 234 models are added in 260-268 ms against 264-265 ms,
  178 distinct meshes both times. The zombie street's frame
  (`tools/ae3d_bench`) is 256 draws, 54,422 triangles, 7 program and 53
  material binds before and after; its GPU passes measured within the
  noise of a card shared with another job (scene 1.27-1.30 ms against
  1.24-1.34 ms) and its CPU work 0.24 against 0.23 ms.
  `test_backend_parity` gives the same numbers, channel for channel, and
  the 55 suites that draw on Vulkan the same results; the Khronos layer
  with synchronization validation reports no error on `test_vk_mesh`,
  `test_backend_parity` and eleven suites that upload meshes.
- `vulkan.c`: 7,038 lines before, 6,906 after.
