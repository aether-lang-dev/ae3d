### Walkers keep clear of a body getting up

- **The way round a body handed over keeps its clearance**
  (`ae3d.locomotion`, `round_body`). A walker whose way crosses a body
  lying or getting up (set_stand_in: the line from its pelvis to its neck)
  was turned toward a point CLEAR past the body's nearer end, along the
  line. Heading there, it cut the corner; and a body getting up has a short
  line, so the point was beside it. In the plaza, walkers walked into the
  knees and hands of the rising, 0.3 to 0.65 m from the line, 104 frames
  in six runs at 24 walkers. Now the walker goes the way that turns least
  of those touching a circle `clear` round either end and keeping `clear`
  of the whole line; already nearer than that, it steps out, straight away
  from it. The same six runs: 6 frames.
  - tests/test_locomotion: a body lying across the way, given, is walked
    round no nearer than the clearance less 5 cm (0.847 m; 0.743 on main);
    a body getting up, its line 4 cm long, at the clearance as well.
