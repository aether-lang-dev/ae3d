### Motion: a car through the plaza -- knocked down, up again, walking on

`tools/plaza_showcase.ae` (#509) now has a car crossing the plaza along
lanes through the crowd, every 6 s at 7 m/s. A walker it reaches is handed
from locomotion to an active ragdoll and back:

- **Struck.** Locomotion is paused and `ae3d.feet` eases out. The figure's
  active ragdoll is built the first time (`motion.on_figure`), and after that
  unparked and stood on its rig. It is POWERED and hit at the hips the way
  the car goes (45 N·s per m/s of the car's speed, and up), and falls.
- **Up.** Once it has got up and stayed up and strong for 0.8 s, the
  walker's object is moved to where its body stands, turned to the way it
  faces, as `ae3d.handover` moves a rig back. Locomotion is placed there,
  and the ragdoll blends back into the clip.
- **Walking on.** When the blend has finished, the ragdoll is parked and
  locomotion takes the walker up again. It stands for 0.4 s while
  `ae3d.feet` eases back in, then walks on.
  - Walking on at once, a held foot only partly kept its turn while the
    feet were still easing in. It swung round with the body, its toe up to
    30 cm over the ground; 22 of the large slides came from that, and 2
    remain.

Locomotion gains `set_paused` and `place`:
- **`set_paused`:** while paused, locomotion takes no input, does not
  step, chooses no clip and writes no hips; its foot hold is let go.
- **`place`:** stands its capsule and figure where a body handed back
  stands.
