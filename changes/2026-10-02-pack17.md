### Pack 17: a child drawn under its parent, menu rows, the world's sounds, audio in the editor

- `model_interpolated_position` and `_rotation` give a model the steps do not
  move, under one they do, where it is drawn: its own transform under its
  parent's drawn one (#565).
- `ae3d.menu`: a slider shows its value in a column of its own, to its
  step's places or as `set_value_text` words, with the knob in a strip
  under the text. `set_label`, and a `choice` widget over named options
  (#568).
- `ae3d.worldsound`: rain, wind and thunder from the weather (thunder after
  the strike's distance over the speed of sound), a car's engine pitched by
  its speed, and physics hits capped at four a frame. All of it is
  synthesised in Aether, no asset files. `ae3d.weather` records each
  strike's distance (#566).
- Audio Source as a component kind: inspector, scene file and agent; muted
  in the editor except while Simulate runs (#567).
