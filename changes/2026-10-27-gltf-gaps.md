### glTF: data: URIs, sparse accessors, and the images they carry (#868)

- A buffer or an image written as a base64 `data:` URI loads (the reader named
  it a warning and dropped the buffer, so the primitive too); the URL-safe
  alphabet, whitespace and missing padding are taken.
- A sparse accessor is made whole when opened: its base values (zeros when it has
  no buffer view, the specification's way to say "mostly nothing"), then the
  sparse elements, for any component type of indices (unsigned byte, short or
  int). Every reader of an accessor sees the result, so positions, normals, UVs,
  indices, inverse bind matrices and animation samplers are all substituted. A
  sparse accessor naming an element it lacks is refused with a warning.
- Images embedded in a `.glb`'s binary chunk already loaded (the header comment
  still said they did not); they are now held by a fixture of their own with a
  normal map beside the base colour, and an image reaching past its buffer is
  refused rather than read.
- `core.mesh_uv_at(mesh, i)` reads a vertex's first UV back.
- Fixtures are written by `tools/make_gltf_gaps_fixture.ae`.
