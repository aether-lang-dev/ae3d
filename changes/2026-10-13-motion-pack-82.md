### A fast car meets a figure's limbs at their surface

- **Travel speculation** (`ae3d.physics`, `physics_set_travel_speculation`;
  aephysics#173, aephysics PR #175). A dynamic car of 1.2 t at 6.5 m/s moves
  11 cm a step, under Box3D's continuous threshold (half its extent), and
  its contacts began at Box3D's 2 cm of speculation. So the first frame of
  a strike found a struck walker's thigh or forearm 5 to 8 cm inside it,
  pushed out over the next 5 to 7 frames: 86% of the plaza scorecard's
  seconds in the car. With the world's travel speculation on, every
  contact keeps speculative points out to the pair's travel over the step,
  and a moving body's bounds cover its next step, so a limb is brought up
  to speed as the gap closes. Off by default, as in Box3D; the plaza turns
  it on.
  - tests/test_car_strike: over 15 strikes (6, 7 and 8 m/s, five lines), a
    limb goes into the car 3.8 cm deep on average with it (4.6 at most),
    6.8 without. Some of a step's depth stays where a limb's joints hold it
    back against the push; a free body comes in under 1 cm (aephysics'
    test_edge_cases).
