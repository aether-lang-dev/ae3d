### Motion: a plaza of walkers, its feet measured

`tools/plaza_showcase.ae` is the first slice of #509's showcase. Like
`motion_showcase`, it runs off CI: ci.sh never builds it.

**The scene:** an 18 m plaza with a ramp up to a deck, three steps and a
kerb.
- Box men wander it, each walking to a place and then picking another,
  some at a run. `AE3D_FIGURES` sets how many (24 by default).
- Each has locomotion with its foot hold, legs solved onto the physics
  ground (`ae3d.feet`), and steps aside for the others (`ae3d.avoid`).

**The measure:** every few seconds it reports each stance's slide. A
stance runs from a foot set down by `ae3d.feet` to that foot lifted; the
slide is how far its toe moved over the ground in that time. It also
reports the frame time.

It reads the feet after the frame is drawn. Read before the pose phase,
they were still the clip's feet, and showed 4.5–13 cm a stance where the
drawn feet slide 0.006 cm.

On an RTX 4070 Ti (Vulkan, vsync at 144 Hz), over 15 s:

| walkers | stances | slide, mean | at most | over 1 cm | frame |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 20 | 0.006 cm | 0.07 cm | 0 | 6.9 ms |
| 24 | 782 | 0.14 cm | 6.5 cm | 23 (3%) | 6.9 ms |
| 200 | 4,516 | 0.42 cm | 11.7 cm | 561 (12%) | 6.9 ms |

200 walkers hold the vsync rate, past #509's 60 fps. The stances over
1 cm come with crowding: figures stepping aside and turning to new goals.

**`ae3d.feet` fix:** a held foot is now let go once the clip lifts it 2 cm
(`LIFT_RELEASE`) above where it was set down.
- Until now it was held until the ankle was 1.35 times its bind-pose
  height off the floor. The box man's planted ankle is 0.07 m high but
  0.1 m in its bind pose, so its walking foot was still held 6 cm up into
  its swing.
- With 24 walkers, the mean slide goes from 0.154 to 0.138 cm and the
  worst from 7.3 to 6.5 cm.
