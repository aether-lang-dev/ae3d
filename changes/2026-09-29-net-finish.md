### Multiplayer finished: fields of a script's state, relevance by sight, the link on the agent channel, authority, smooth corrections, and the street played by sixteen

- `net.replicate` replicates a field of a script's state beside an object's transform (#486): an int, a float in steps of a precision or as its 64 bits, a bool, a Vec3, a text of up to 31 bytes the session keeps (`set_text`, `text`). The host reads each every tick; a field goes in its object's record against what the client acknowledged, only when it changes -- a mask and each change as a zigzag varint -- and whole, with its kinds and precisions, where the client has none, so a client reads an object's fields before the reliable word that makes it. A client writes every field from every snapshot it takes, never from one older than it applied, and `on_changed` calls a hook once a change, in tick order; `fields_known` says what it holds.
  - `tests/test_net_fields.ae`, over loopback UDP at 50 ± 20 ms and 10% lost: 49,676 field values of twelve crates, a mover, three players and a rocket against the host's at each frame's snapshot tick, none different, bit for bit; 564 changes, 564 hooks on each client in order, none missed or extra; a client's own player's health reaches it; within a budget of 120 bytes a field and its transform arrive in one snapshot every time; six still fields on twenty moving objects cost the same bytes to the byte as none.
- Relevance by what each client can see (#487): a client sends its view (`set_view`, `set_camera_view`) with its inputs, 18 bytes when it has moved 25 cm or turned a degree; the host judges each object against the frustum and, inside it, a ray from the eye against its static world (`set_occluders`, `physics.ray_static_hits`), a ray an object every sixth tick. What a client sees gathers priority four times as fast, what it cannot a quarter as fast; a seen object that waited a tick goes ahead of the rest, and so does anything that waited a second and a half. A record no budget's room could hold goes alone rather than wait for ever.
  - `tests/test_net_sight.ae`, 500 moving objects in a street of buildings, two clients looking opposite ways, 900 bytes a snapshot: what a client sees waits a tick at most (4 by distance alone), what it cannot 18 ticks at most and none left unsent; turning round, the 125 objects newly in view all sent within a tick; 69% of the bytes on what is seen, against 25% by distance alone; 228 rays a tick for two clients.
- The link measured on every transport, both ways (#488): ten pings a second give the round trip and its jitter (RFC 6298, integer microseconds) and the loss over the last fifty; every byte counted by peer, kind and way, with rates over windows of a second (`rate`, `link_bytes`, `round_trip_us`, `jitter_us`, `loss_ppm`, `budget_of`, `worst_wait_any`, `correction_um`). UDP counts each connection's headers and resends apart (`netudp.conn_headers`, `conn_resent`).
  - `net.stats` and `net.set_link` on the agent channel, in the engine's command table and answered by `ae3d.net` through `agent.provide`; every figure a whole number in the unit its name says. `tests/test_agent_net.ae` asks a host and a client over the channel, held still, and compares all 114 figures with the sessions' own, twice: none differs; `net.set_link` from 20 ms to 100 ms moves the reported round trip by 155 to 165 ms for the 160 added.
- Who simulates what (#413): a script's `fixed_update` runs only where its object is simulated -- on the host for what it owns, on the owning client for its player -- the engine passing a remote object's by (`behaviour.object_remote`); a dynamic body on a client's copy is kinematic, driven where the session puts it (`physics.rigidbody_set_kind`); `net.release` gives them back. A remote object is corrected smoothly: after the view ran past its newest sample, it is drawn on from where it stood, its error falling to a third every tenth of a second (`set_smoothing`), never snapped.
  - `tests/test_net_authority.ae`: a crate's script's `fixed_update` 240 times on the host and none on either client, its `update` on all three; each player's only on its own client; the crate's body kinematic on a client, within 0.05 mm of the host's as it is pushed; through 400 ms of lost snapshots an object at 6 m/s moves 0.40 m in a step at most where it snapped 2.06 m, and is 0.65 mm off 0.8 s later.
- A command goes as its change in millimetres a second from the one before it, predicted as the wire carries it: a walk turning every step costs 1.7 KB a second where the floats cost 4.5, `tests/test_players.ae`'s client 0.97 where it cost 1.47. A client with a player waits for its next input to acknowledge a snapshot. Protocol 6.
- `examples/net_street.ae`: the zombie street played by a host and fifteen bot clients over loopback UDP, 50 ms each way and 2% lost (#489) -- players on scripted paths, two cars with their speed, horn and driver as fields, a horde of 400 of the pipeline's zombie simulated on every peer and shoved by the cars; `AE3D_NET=host` and `join:addr` across machines. `ci.sh` runs ten seconds of it: every client under 20 KB a second down (7.3 to 11.5 in its worst second, over eight runs) and 4 up (3.1 to 3.2), every client's horde the host's at each of the 4,300 ticks compared a run and the 4,600 seals checked, the cars' fields right on all fifteen; the worst correction 3 µm; the host's own work 0.05 ms a frame alone and 0.14 with fifteen clients.
- docs/networking.md: fields, sight, who simulates what, the smoothing, the link measured, the street and every measurement; docs/agent.md (through tools/agent_doc_text.md): a multiplayer session.
