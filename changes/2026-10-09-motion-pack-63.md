### Motion: a face-down writher writhes where it lies

- **Face down, the legs bend at the knee and the arms stay tucked (#691).**
  A figure shoved down from within 10 degrees of straight behind lands face
  down, and writhing there it drew one knee up under its belly at the hip,
  as a writher on its back does. The knee went into the ground, lifted that
  side, and rolled the figure onto its back in 1.3 s, its pelvis carried
  0.3 m and more across the ground. Face down the legs now bend at the knee
  (1.0 rad at the top), the heels coming up behind, with the hips at 0.15.
  - The folded arms had all their muscle, and face down that pressed the
    chest up off the ground; the kicking legs then pushed the body along on
    them. Either alone moved a face-down writher 1 to 1.5 cm on average;
    the two together 7 cm, and 0.3 m in 4 of 64. Face down the arms now
    have 40% of their muscle: tucked, not pushing.
  - `tests/test_writhe.ae`, 64 writhers from 32 ways: every one drifts under
    0.3 m (62 before) and 10 cm at the most (0.36 m). Each one's leg swings
    at least 0.31 rad, at the hip or, face down, at the knee. The suite
    measures both, since a writher on its side counts face up or face down
    from one moment to the next.
