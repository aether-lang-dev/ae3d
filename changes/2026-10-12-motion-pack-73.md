- **Clips for the other ways** (#672, `ae3d.locomotion`). A figure walked
  by locomotion played its idle, walk or run by speed, whatever way its
  body went. Stepping round another in a crowd, its body went sideways
  while it faced on, so it walked forward while its capsule slid: its feet
  skated, and its knees swung into the one it stepped round. In the
  car-free plaza, 76 of 78 meetings, two walkers first found inside each
  other, had one going 40 degrees or more off the way it faced, the other
  mostly straight ahead of it. Now `set_directions(l, back, left, right)`
  gives it clips that go back and to either side. Each one's way from the
  walk's, and its pace, are read off the clip as the walk's are (the way
  its feet go back under it, what they cover over a cycle). The clip played
  is the one whose way, from where the figure faces, is nearest the way
  its body goes, at its own pace for the body's speed. It is kept until
  another is DIRECTION_KEEP (0.2 rad) nearer, and is not played past
  DIRECTION_PACE (twice) its own pace: faster than that a person turns and
  walks.
