### Destroying a figure takes the figure with it

- **`engine.destroy` on a figure's object takes the figure and its motion
  with it (#714).** Destroying the object took out only its group. The
  figure's meshes stayed in the renderer, posed through a skeleton whose
  roots hung from the freed group; the figure's scene record read the
  freed object; and an active ragdoll on it stepped on, dressed on the
  freed skeleton. Siltreach's colosseum, disposing its fighters between
  bouts, segfaulted in the shadow pass once that memory was used again.
  - The figure's script has a destroy phase: its meshes out of the
    renderer, the figure out of the engine's list, the figure and its
    file's models freed.
  - `motion.on_figure` and `on_skeleton` put a script on the object whose
    destroy phase takes the ragdoll out (`physics.ragdoll_destroy`, new:
    its twelve bones' objects destroyed, its bodies out of the world) and
    releases the motion. `motion_free` takes the phase off an object that
    still stands.
  - The scene frees what destroy phases destroy in the same pass. Counted
    once at the start, the ragdoll's bones were cleared with the list and
    never freed.
  - While the whole scene goes at once (`scene_clear`, `scene_free`), the
    phases leave things to their owners: a program frees its figures and
    motions after the engine, as before.
  - `tests/test_figure.ae`: a third figure destroyed ten frames in has none
    of its meshes drawn after (one before; one after without the fix).
    `tests/test_motion_figure.ae`: a second box man on `on_figure`,
    destroyed, leaves the scene with its twelve bones (13 objects; 1
    without the fix) and none of its meshes is drawn after.
