### The Vulkan device made in Aether

- The loader, the instance, the window's surface, the pick of the
  physical device and the logical device with its queues move from
  `native/gpu/vulkan.c` to `ae3d.vkdevice` (#402), on `contrib.vulkan.vk`:
  its loader, its `load_instance_with` (the one vkGetInstanceProcAddr the
  whole process goes through, Streamline's interposer when DLSS is asked
  for) and its `surface_create` over the window's native handle -- the
  HWND on Windows, the CAMetalLayer on macOS, the X11 window elsewhere.
- The same choices as before: Vulkan 1.2 where the loader has it, 1.1
  otherwise; portability enumeration and subset where offered; the rays
  where the device has the four extensions and three features and
  `AE3D_NO_RAYS` is not set; independent blending on. The backend adopts
  what is made (`ae3d_vk_adopt_device`), loads its dispatch through the
  same entry point, and the device is destroyed after the backend has
  let go of it.
- `ae3d.vkhost`, `ae3d.vkmeter`, `ae3d.vkoverlay` and `ae3d.vktexture`
  read the device from `ae3d.vkdevice`; the backend's handle getters are
  gone.
- A top-level `when` in an imported module loses its externs
  (aether-lang-dev/aether#2275): the platform externs are declared for
  every platform and each call is gated by a statement-level `when`.
- Measured on an RTX 4070 Ti, the C then the Aether, back to back: the
  offscreen renderer starts in 171.6 ms against 166.7 ms (median of 5,
  the C 165.5-179.9 ms, the Aether 166.2-167.1 ms).
  `test_backend_parity` gives the same numbers, channel for channel, and
  the 55 suites that draw on Vulkan pass, windowed ones included; the
  Khronos layer with synchronization validation reports nothing new on
  twelve of them (`test_engine_order`'s five layout errors are the C's
  as well).
- `vulkan.c`: 6,906 lines before, 6,490 after.
