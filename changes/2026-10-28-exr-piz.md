### OpenEXR PIZ files load as environment maps (#875 follow-up)

- `ae3d.exrpiz`, the PIZ block decoder of `ae3d.openexr`: a block's Huffman stream, the per-channel 2-D wavelet
  (the 14-bit and the 16-bit forms) and the value table from its bitmap, back to the scanlines the other
  compressions leave, for HALF and FLOAT channels, RGB, RGBA and a lone Y, any data window. Pure Aether, from the
  format's documentation and the structure of the OpenEXR library's decoder (BSD-3 notice in the module header).
  Every Poly Haven EXR is PIZ, so `engine_set_environment` and `AE3D_ENV` now take them.
- Still refused by name: PXR24, B44, B44A, DWAA, DWAB, tiled, deep and multipart files.
- `half_to_float` keeps a half's negative zero a negative zero (it was +0), so a decode equals OpenEXR's bit for bit.
- Tests: `test_envimage` holds 24 PIZ files written by the OpenEXR library, and two Poly Haven 1k assets, to
  that library's own decode, bit for bit (a hash of the texel bits), and cuts and damages the small ones at every
  byte; `tools/exr_reference/exrref.cpp` writes and dumps them (a development tool, not built, not linked).
