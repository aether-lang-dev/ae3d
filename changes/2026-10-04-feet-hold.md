### Feet: a planted foot keeps its turn and its hold, and the body walks its arc

Measured on the box man with its legs solved onto the ground by `ae3d.feet`
in place of locomotion's own foot hold, walked straight, stopped and turned
round (#611).

- **The foot kept as the clip turned it.** After a leg's two-bone solve the
  foot hung from the solved shin and was carried round with it: a planted
  foot pitched about its locked ankle and swept its toe over the ground,
  55% of the body's way walking straight. The foot now gets back the

- **A planted foot keeps the turn it was set down with,** the body turning
  over it, and is eased from it to the clip's over its release like its

  clip lifts it, or the body is `LOCK_REACH` (0.35 m) from it.
