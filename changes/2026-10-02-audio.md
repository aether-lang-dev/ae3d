### Audio: sources on game objects, the listener, buses (#415)

- `ae3d.audio` over std.audio's mixer. An AudioSource component plays a
  clip (WAV, MP3, FLAC) through a bus. Every frame each spatial source is
  placed against the listener (the camera, or `set_listener`'s object):
  inverse-distance gain between `min_distance` and `max_distance`,
  equal-power pan, and a Doppler pitch from the two velocities.
- Buses: MUSIC, EFFECTS, AMBIENCE and VOICE with volumes, plus `duck` (one
  bus lowered while another plays).
- `AE3D_AUDIO=0`, or no device, is silent. `AE3D_AUDIO=headless` /
  `attach_headless` gives a mix a test renders.
- `engine_update` (an engine driven by its owner, the editor's) now runs
  the pose phase and the late updates as the loop does.
- `tests/test_audio.ae`: distance, pan, Doppler, buses, mixing and ducking
  match their formulas in the rendered mix. 258 sources are placed in
  0.023 ms a frame.
- Docs: `docs/audio.md`. World sounds are #566, the editor is #567, and OGG
  is aether#2364.
