### Aether 0.743: slices

- The engine builds on Aether 0.743, where `T[]` is a slice, a pointer and
  its length (aether-lang-dev/aether#1286). aephysics' clip buffers swap as
  slices (aether-lang-dev/aephysics#54). The editor's row handles are the
  allocation itself rather than a slice cast back to a pointer
  (aether-lang-dev/aether#2304).
- Scripts call the runtime's bounds checks, so they need the program's
  runtime. The one-runtime-per-process linking in the script-components
  entry covers them.
- Windows needs aether-lang-dev/aether#2305. Until it lands, 0.743 writes
  the source path into the checks with unescaped backslashes, and a path
  under `C:\Users` does not compile.
