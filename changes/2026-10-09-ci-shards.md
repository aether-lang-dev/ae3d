### CI: every tier split over runners; the fly camera's look through ae3d.input

- **CI's tiers are split over runners at once (#710).** `AE3D_CI_SHARD=k/n`
  hands each runner every n-th suite (the first shard takes fewer, with the
  steps that run once); the workflow runs the suites over four Linux
  runners, the apps over two (the examples, the editor), the leak check
  over two Macs. A full rebuild had the Linux suites' runner at 31 to 36
  minutes (166 programs built in 1438 s on e8de382's run), the whole run's
  critical path; each shard now builds 32 to 45 of them. The job limit goes
  from 40 to 25 minutes.
- **The fly camera's mouse look reads the cursor through ae3d.input (#724).**
  It read the window's cursor, so an injected cursor turned nothing: a
  scripted session could fly the camera but never look round.
  `tests/test_fly_look.ae`: a cursor injected 240 px across with the look
  held turns the camera 22.8 degrees (0 before), and nothing with it up.
- **`ae3d.voxel` imports `std.string`, which it calls (#730):** Aether 0.799
  holds a module to the modules it imports itself.
- **`test_engine_shadows` reads its frame at the framebuffer's size (#671):**
  on a HiDPI screen it read the bottom-left quarter, flat ground, and
  failed; it reads 79.8% darker under the shadow on an M1 Pro now.
