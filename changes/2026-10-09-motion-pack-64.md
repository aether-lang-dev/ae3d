### Motion: a fitted figure gets a person's ranges, measured on itself

- **A figure's joint limits are measured on the figure it was fitted to
  (#706, #704).** The limits were measured on aephysics' reference figure,
  and fitting kept each joint's limits as they were on its parent. An MPFB
  rig's pelvis bone runs 20 to 32.5 degrees up and back from the hips, the
  pelvis body is fitted along it, and each hip's cone, kept on the pelvis,
  leaned that far forward of the figure. On Siltreach's scavenger a thigh
  hanging straight down was 16 degrees past it: standing on straight legs,
  POWERED and untouched, its pelvis was tipped 0.22 rad by its own limits,
  and a 0.29 m/s jab to the neck felled it in a second. `fitted_shape` now
  measures `human_anatomical_limits` on the fitted shape, as aephysics
  documents it ("so a fitted shape takes them as well as the reference's").
  - Siltreach's scavenger: tipped 0.031 rad standing (0.219), and the jab
    leaves it standing, 0.099 rad at the most and 0.016 0.5 s on (fell,
    0.58 rad at 0.75 s). The raider stood before and stands now.
  - Even a straight rig's hip cone sat 8.5 degrees forward of a person's:
    a thigh hanging down was 58.7 degrees from its centre, where 50 is
    meant, which allowed 16.5 degrees back and 133 ahead for 25 and 125.
    Now 50.5. The shoulders' centres move about 6 degrees.
  - `tests/test_ragdoll_fit.ae` adds a rig whose pelvis runs 32.5 degrees
    back: a thigh straight down is 22.8 degrees inside each hip's cone
    (16 past before), one swung 20 degrees back 4 inside (35 past), and
    powered it stands through every push with its pelvis within 0.05 rad
    (before, it fell standing still, 1.8 rad).
- **A crawler pulls on both forearms at once, each only until its
  shoulder comes over its elbow (#625).** A crawl moves on its forearms'
  grip, the chest carried on them. Pulled on one forearm, the body was
  levered up onto that arm and over onto its side, rolled back onto its
  front and pulled over again, in place; pulled on with the shoulder past
  the elbow, the chest was driven up over a straight arm (1.3 m/s, the
  shoulders 0.14 to 0.53 m in 0.3 s) and onto its back. Over 165 strikes
  from the side (the suite's 55 and two sets shifted 0.125 and 0.0625 m/s
  and 5 degrees): 94 crawl (main 94), and of 41 landing off their front
  31 end on it (main 17 of 35). With the hips' 25 degrees back a figure
  that does not crawl heaves in place more: 105 get half their path along
  the way (main 124), 109 with the new limits and the old pull. The
  crawl's propulsion rides on the chest lifted onto the arms; a crawl that
  carries it low is #625's.
- **The get-up suite reads where the feet lay at the ankles.** It read
  them at the calf bodies' origins, the knees. A figure lying with one knee
  raised and its shins fallen to a side rose facing its ankles within half
  a degree, and its knees 30 to 38 degrees off: over 21 knock-backs main's
  rises faced their ankles within 0.5 degrees and their knees 1 to 30
  (the suite's 29.67 against its 30 was chance). The check is now 5
  degrees, at the ankles.
- **Ragdolls with ankles, slices 1 and 2 (#709).** The rig's root has its
  own slot after the most bones a ragdoll can have, and every loop runs to
  the ragdoll's own bone count; no ragdoll has ankles yet, and the motion
  gate's numbers are the same to the last digit.
- **aephysics c327210**, its latest: the pairs phase at 1.1 to 1.2x Box3D
  (aephysics#158; the motion gate's numbers the same to the last digit)
  and world snapshots (aephysics#149, first part).
- **CI stays on Aether 0.791.0.** 0.796.0 fixes aether#2584 and #2585, and
  the motion gate (33 of 33, the same numbers) and the editor build on it,
  but its macOS leak check still fails: aether#2586, in test_components,
  test_scene_io, test_audio and test_worldsound.
- **A program is compiled with the include directories its generated C
  names (aether#1986).** The generated C includes a module's header by the
  name its `@c_include` gives and lists the module's directory as
  `// aether-include:`, which `ae build` puts on the include path. build.sh
  and build_script.sh read the `// aether-source:` lines and not these, so
  a header was found only where a `-I` happened to point at its module. On
  0.796 contrib.vulkan.vk includes `"../aether_vulkan_compat.h"`, and
  every program that draws failed to compile, the editor too
  (editor/build_editor.sh reads them now as well).
- **Windows builds no longer race on the runtime DLL (#692).** It was
  rebuilt whenever the toolchain's `libaether.a` was newer than it, so
  builds started together after a toolchain change all rebuilt it over each
  other through one scratch file; an older toolchain switched to kept a
  newer one's runtime; and 0.796's archive is dated hours ahead of the
  clock, so every build found it stale. A stamp beside the DLL names the
  archive it was built from, and one build relinks it under a lock into
  names of its own; eight suites built at once against a missing DLL all
  link, the DLL built once. ci.sh checks the stamp test and the include
  reader on every host.
