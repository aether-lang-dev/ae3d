### Wounds on a model, in its bind space (#543)

- `core.model_set_wound(m, index, centre, radii, rotation)`: up to sixteen
  ellipsoids in a model's bind space. Inside one the surface is drawn cut
  down through four layers (blood, fat, muscle, bone by default;
  `model_set_wound_layer` sets each one's colour and the share of the radius
  it starts at), and inside `model_set_wound_core` of the radius it is a
  hole -- in the scene, its depth and every shadow, the same GLSL in the
  scene's fragment shader and the depth one. `model_set_wound_count` and
  `model_clear_wounds` take them away.
- Held on both renderers: a wound on a skinned column puts blood on 172
  pixels where there were none and holes it through to its inside; cleared,
  the column is drawn exactly as before; a wound through a sphere holes its
  shadow (1,228 of 1,340 pixels). zombie_city at 20,000 costs what it cost.
