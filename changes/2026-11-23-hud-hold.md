### A held frame keeps its HUD

- The engine cleared the HUD after every frame it drew, and the scripts'
  updates drew it again. A held frame (`frame.pause`, `AE3D_HOLD`) runs no
  update, so it showed no HUD. The agent's `snapshot` of a paused scene had
  none, where `AE3D_SNAPSHOT` of a running one did (Hushlight's #14).
- Now each frame's start lets the last frame's drawing go and keeps what a
  late update drew after it, so the next frame shows it as before. A held
  frame keeps instead what its scripts drew last, with its menus and late
  updates over it let go each frame.
  - `hud.mark`, `hud.back_to` and `hud.drop_before` do this for the
    screen's drawing and the world's text (`worldtext.truncate`,
    `worldtext.drop_before`).
- `test_hud_hold`, new: a held frame keeps the scripts' panel and the late
  update's mark, 2 quads on the first held frame read and on the sixth.
