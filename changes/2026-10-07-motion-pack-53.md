### Motion: clips made at run time, parts on their muscles, walkers who meet, skinned motion vectors

- **A blended clip no longer tears the figure (#650).** A figure keeps each
  frame's clip pose before a transition's offsets and its layers go on.
  A node the clip does not key starts the next frame from that pose, if
  nothing else has moved it since the figure drew it. Before, offsets
  summed frame after frame on such nodes: a clip keying only turns,
  blended in out of the rest pose, ended 474 m off the cut's pose. A figure
  also counts as drawn in its rest pose until a clip plays, so a first
  blend no longer carries each bone from the origin (a bone stretched
  49 m).
- **Clips made at run time (#645).** `figure.add_clip(f, animation, clip,
  node)` gives a figure a clip it was not loaded with: captured,
  retargeted or generated, one `anim` clip a node, named
  `"<animation>:<node>"`. It is counted, named, played, blended, layered
  and matched as the file's clips are (`tests/test_runtime_clip.ae`).
- **Motion matching for a game's own animator (#636).**
  `matching.answer(m, at, velocity, wanted, heading, clock, delta)` searches
  and says what to play (`playing_clip`, `playing_time`, `pace`) without
  posing the figure. Run-time clips enter the database through
  `figure.add_clip`. On the Fox it answers a walk to a body walking, and
  the figure stays on the frame it was given (`tests/test_matching.ae`).
- **A blow's cost is the game's to say (#653).** `motion.hit(m, bone,
  point, impulse, push, cost)` takes the share of the struck bone's muscle
  the blow costs, half that of its neighbours'. A jab to the head at 0.1
  takes 9.5% of the neck's muscle where the default, the speed on the
  3.6 kg neck alone, took 95% and dropped the head toward the fist.
- **Parts on their own muscles while the figure follows its clip (#658).**
  `set_part_mode(m, PART_SPINE, POWERED)` on an `ANIMATED` figure now does
  something:
  - the part's bodies and what hangs from them leave their anchors and
    are driven toward the clip by their joints' muscles, the clip's turn
    handed to physics in the pose phase;
  - they are drawn as their joints bend, from the parent as drawn;
  - the pelvis and legs stay on the clip.

  Jabbed in the face, the head snaps back 3.3 cm and is home by 0.8 s.
  The figure stays `ANIMATED`, and the hand back is seamless
  (`tests/test_part_powered.ae`). The snap hardly grows with the blow's
  cost, because a muscle is a velocity servo, rigid within its torque
  (#661).
- **Walkers no longer walk through each other (#662).** A character
  controller's move meets the other solid controllers near it as
  collision planes, and the two slide round each other. A walker handed to
  its ragdoll stops being solid (`locomotion.set_paused`), so its capsule
  doesn't stand as an invisible post where it was struck. Two walkers head
  on stay their two radii apart, within the mover's 5 mm slop, and pass
  (`tests/test_characters_meet.ae`).
- **Skinned figures write their own motion vectors (#639).** The previous
  frame's palette goes to the vertex shader as each bone's top three
  rows, on both backends. Before, a skinned draw read only its model's
  motion, a swinging limb read as still, and TAA smeared it into a ghost.
  A figure carried 40 cm by its hips now reads 29.4 px against a
  projected 29.0, where it read 0 (`tests/test_velocity.ae`).
- aephysics moved to its latest main (81254d6: body states, manifolds
  and contact caches in float).
