  is called for** (#750, `ae3d.motion`). Shoved from behind at 1.0 m/s, a
  person's walking pace, the box man could take no step for 1.4 s. The
  assist slowed it under STEP_SPEED and held its centre of mass past its
  toes as it crept over them at 0.15 to 0.33 m/s: its capture point 11 to
  13 cm past them, under STEP_MARGIN, its centre of mass 29 cm ahead of its
  ankles, leaning 0.31 rad. Its step came too late and it fell. The assist
  faded only for a trunk nearly still; it now also fades for a body

  its capture point is within STEP_MARGIN, the gap neither rule covered.

  anything but its feet (a wall, a car, another figure), which its feet are
  not all that hold.
