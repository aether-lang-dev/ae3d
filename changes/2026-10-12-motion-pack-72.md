### A foot let go is carried over the ground, not dragged along it

- **The let-go arc** (`ae3d.feet`). A held foot is let go as the clip
  lifts it, or once the body is `LOCK_REACH` from it, and carried over
  `UNLOCK_SECONDS` from where it was held to where the clip has it. It was
  carried at the clip's height, and the clip has just begun to lift it:
  its toe was still on the ground, and it slid the whole way there. Now it
  is lifted along an arc by `RELEASE_LIFT` (a fifth) of the way it has to
  go, at most `STEP_LIFT`, as the foot's own step is, and set down where
  the clip has it.
  - Why the way is long: in the plaza with no car, a walker's foot is let
    go 9 cm on average from where the walk has it, 5.6 cm along the way the
    body went (4.9 of it ahead) and 5.5 across, turning; 8% only at
    `LOCK_REACH`, 37 cm off. The forward part is the walk's own glide: in
    the clip its foot moves on with the body for the frames it comes down
    and goes up, 3.9 cm each way, where a held foot stays (#759, filed).
  - tests/test_feet: a foot held while the body went on 10 cm over it, then
    its heel lifted 3.3 cm by the clip, is let go 19 cm from where the clip
    has it, and carried up to 3.8 cm over the clip's height, 2.1 cm of the
    way within 0.5 cm of it; it comes down on the clip's ankle. Carried at
    the clip's height, all 19 cm were along the ground.
