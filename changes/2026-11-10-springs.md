### Secondary motion: spring chains and jiggle (#989)

- **Spring chains** (`ae3d.springs`, `animator.springs_of`): a chain of
  joints (`chain_add`, root first; `chain_add_tree` for a joint and all
  under it), each turned to aim its tail at a point mass carried through
  the world by the figure and pulled to where the pose puts it: a spring
  (`stiffness`, 1/s^2), `damping` against its speed relative to the pose's,
  `drag` against the air's (`set_wind`), gravity, by semi-implicit Euler
  with the damping implicit, held at its length from its head. Jiggle is a
  chain of one joint. Pulled to the pose rather than to the parent's turned
  frame (VRM's way), every mode of a chain dies down at (damping + drag) / 2:
  pulled to the parent, a ponytail of four rang 10 cm five seconds after a
  stop. A ponytail on a figure that runs off at 4 m/s, turns at 1.5 rad/s
  and stops: its tip trails 87 mm running off and swings 102 mm out of the
  turn; its swing dies down with a time constant of 0.53 s against
  2 / (damping + drag) = 0.5 s (0.75 s with gravity, lying on the back);
  still within 0.14 mm at rest, and within 0.002 mm of its pose with no
  gravity.
- **Colliders** (`collider_add`): spheres and capsules on the body's joints
  in groups, a chain held out of the groups its `groups` bits name, each
  tail grown by its hit radius. A tail is put onto the cone of directions
  from its head that clear a collider, exactly (a capsule about its nearest
  point, up to four passes): no joint of the ponytail ever inside past its
  hit radius (2e-5 mm at the deepest, rounding), no point between joints
  inside the colliders themselves.
- **In the animator**: solved last in each evaluation, after the IK, on the
  fixed step's job pool with the graph, the frames between steps drawn
  between the steps' poses; off between evaluations at a slower rate
  (`set_every`, ae3d.animlod), each tail on its pose, so a figure brought
  back to every step starts them from where it is drawn; let go on
  `resume` and `take_over`. The same to the bit run to run and with the
  pool off. 140 ns a joint a step (eight chains of four joints, two
  colliders).
- **VRM spring bones** (`gltf.spring_count`, `springs.from_scene`,
  `animator.springs_from_file`, on by default for `character.humanoid`):
  VRM 0.x's `secondaryAnimation` and VRM 1.0's `VRMC_springBone` read with
  their colliders, their 60 Hz numbers turned into the springs' (a step's
  stiffness s on a joint l long is s / (step l) per second squared, a drag d
  is -ln(1 - d) / step). VRoid Studio's base female: its bust and skirt, 8
  chains, and 6 spheres on its thighs; run round the same course in the
  engine, a skirt's back trails 27 mm, no tail inside a thigh's collider,
  at rest within 0.12 mm.
