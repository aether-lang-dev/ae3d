### A struck figure's muscles start from where its bodies are

- **The joints' targets ease from the bodies' pose onto the clip's over
  the hand-over** (#678). A figure handed to POWERED from the pose on the
  screen has its bodies put on that pose, and the drawing eases from it
  onto the bodies over POWER_BLEND. The muscles' targets did not: they
  went onto the clip's pose at once. Whatever the screen had over the
  clip was taken off the targets in one step: a look's turn toward the
  car, the feet's solve. A walker struck while watching the car had its
  neck snap back 6 to 21 degrees, turning 190 to 660 degrees a second
  against its chest in the first three frames.
  - At the first step after a hand-over, each joint's turn from the
    clip's target to where its bodies are (and the pelvis's, in the world)
    is kept and faded out of the targets over POWER_BLEND, with the same
    easing as the drawing. It is taken at that step's targets, not at the
    call: a look or the feet let go after the call, and the targets read
    at the call still had them.
  - In the plaza (seeds 1 and 2, 24 walkers), the neck's turn in the
    first three frames after a strike falls from 490 degrees a second on
    average (46 of 51 strikes over 100) to 53 (5 of 44).
  - test_lookat: the box man looking 60 degrees aside, struck as the
    plaza strikes. Its neck turns 10.6 degrees a second in the three steps
    after (478 on main), and a second on it is on its clip's target.

### Ankles behind an opt-in (#709, slice 3)

- **`physics_set_ankles(p, true)` before ragdolls are made gives them feet
  of their own** (aephysics#152). Each foot is a bone on a joint below its
  calf (toes down 50 degrees and up 35, the sole rolling 15 either way)
  instead of a shape the calf carries rigidly. Off by default; without it
  every figure is the twelve bones, as before.
  - Fitted to a rig, a figure keeps its ankles where the rig has toes to
    stand a foot on, and otherwise goes back to twelve and drops the feet's
    objects.
  - Dressed, each rig's ankle is mapped onto its foot's body.
    `toe_on_ground`, which turned a drawn foot up out of the ground because
    the calf carried it rigidly, leaves a foot with its own body alone.
  - The soles' rolling resistance goes on the foot's own shape. The
    figure's facing at dressing is read from the feet's bodies.
  - In ae3d.motion, the feet have an ankle's muscle (120 N m), `foot_down`
    reads the foot's sole, the crawl's grip holds the feet, and each leg
    part takes its foot (`part_size`, `part_bone`).
  - test_ragdoll_fit: the box man with ankles has fourteen bones, its rig's
    ankles ride its feet's bodies, and POWERED it stands three seconds on
    soles each within 5 mm of level (0.66 mm at most). Without them it is
    twelve.
  - Crouched (hips 25 degrees forward, knees bent 50, ankles 25, POWERED),
    the box man with ankles stands on flat soles, their ends 0.07 mm apart
    in height, its pelvis down to 0.91 m. Without them each foot tilts with
    its shin and it stands on one end of each sole, the other 6.4 cm up,
    its pelvis held at 0.96 m. That is #709's crouch measure.
  - The motion gate's deterministic suites print the same numbers as
    before; the figures without ankles are unchanged.
  - Slice 4, motion on its ankles (a planted foot held flat while the shin
    moves over it, the swing landing the foot level, get-up keys placing
    the feet), comes next, measured on a crouch.
