### A leg near straight bends the way its knee flexes, its foot as the clip has it

- **The two-bone solve turns a limb near straight the short way onto the
  pole's plane** (`ae3d.ik`, `solve_two_bone`, #781). It turned the limb's
  present bend plane onto the pole's, whatever its bend. A limb near
  straight says nothing of its bend by its plane: a knee 1 mm before the
  hip-ankle line and 1 mm behind it, or one a clip overstraightens a few
  degrees, give opposite planes, so the limb was turned 0 degrees one frame
  and 180 the next about its own line, the foot on its tip with it. A
  walk's toe swung 23 cm in a frame at a heel strike, one of the bone snaps
  that failed Siltreach's play gate. Under STRAIGHT_BEND (a knee within
  about 14 degrees of straight) it now takes whichever of the two planes is
  the nearer the pole's, never turned past a quarter round, and the bend
  that follows puts the knee on the pole's side.
  - tests/test_ik: a leg all but straight, its knee 1 mm before the line
    and then 1 mm behind it, keeps its toe 14 cm ahead of the ankle both
    times, its knee toward the pole (16 cm behind it the second time
    before).
- **A walker's knee pole is learnt from real flexes only, shared by its
  legs** (`ae3d.feet`). Its pole came from its own bend until a front was
  learnt, and a front was learnt from any bend past 1 cm. A clip that
  starts a walk with one knee overstraightened set that knee's pole behind
  it: the knee bent backwards with its thigh facing ahead (with the short
  turn above), or its thigh turned half round to face the pole (before). It
  is learnt now from bends past FLEXED (5 cm off the line, past what a clip
  overstraightens a knee), every leg before any is solved, and a leg not
  yet seen flexed takes the way the figure's others flex.
  - tests/test_feet: a figure walking along its own x, one knee flexed
    8 cm ahead, the other 2 cm behind the line with its foot on a 6 cm
    step: that knee bends 12 cm ahead, its thigh still facing ahead. On
    main it bent 15.8 cm backwards; with the short turn alone, the thigh
    turned half round.
- The plaza, 24 runs (seeds 1 to 8 at 20, 24 and 28 walkers) against main:
  knees backwards held at main's (0.13 s against 0.12; the short turn
  without the pole's fix: 168 s, 13 of 24 walkers in their first second).
