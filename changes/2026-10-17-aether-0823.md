### Aether 0.823.0

- **`AETHER_REF` v0.823.0.** A string field read in a loop as an argument
  beside a call (`val_set(c, bd.name, val_copy(bd.val))`) is now released
  once a pass. 0.822.0 released it twice, once at the end of the pass and
  again at the start of the next, so a struct that kept the name held
  freed memory. `ae3d.gpu`'s `val_copy` had copied each field in a function
  of its own (`copy_field`) to avoid that. It copies them in its loop again,
  and `copy_field` is gone. Built with 0.822.0, the loop fails
  `test_gpu_structs` (1 of 36 checks) and `test_gpu_skydome` (2 of 46).
  With 0.823.0 both pass, and `leaks` finds nothing.
