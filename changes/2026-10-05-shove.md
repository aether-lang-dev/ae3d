### Motion: a shove is not a blow

`motion.shove(m, bone, impulse)` pushes `impulse` into a bone over 0.15 s,
as a hand, a shoulder or a bumper pushes (#606). `motion.hit` strikes it
in an instant, as a blow or a bullet lands.

- **No fold.** A blow to the chest gives the chest several times the speed
  it gives the body, and the hips fold under it. Pushed from behind at
  1.0 m/s over the body, the box man's spine folds 0.65 rad struck and
  0.004 rad shoved (`tests/test_shove.ae`).
- **The arms answer it** as they answer a blow (the balance arms and the
  startle), and **it costs no muscle**, where a blow takes the struck
  bone's.
- **The feet hold some back.** Planted, they push against a shove while it
  lasts, so the body comes out at about 0.75 of the speed a blow gives it.
  A person pushed does the same.

The motion showcase now shoves its figures. Pushed 2.4 m/s at a wall, the
bracing figure braces and stays up, where struck it doubled over and slid
down the wall. Pushed 2.9 m/s from behind, the falling figure reaches
forward and goes down onto its knees and hands. Struck, it dived flat
through the air.
