### The game's UI: layout, images, input, menus, and the editor's viewport

- `ae3d.ui2d` (#459, #484): a game's UI that stays -- layers, panels that
  clip and scroll, rectangles and bars with a fill, text wrapped and
  aligned, images, buttons, toggles, sliders and text fields -- placed by
  nine anchors and offsets in the UI's units, which are pixels times the
  monitor's content scale (or the game's), every edge rounded to a whole
  pixel. The engine makes one (`engine.engine_ui`) and draws it into its
  overlay after what scripts drew, at the size the frame is drawn at; a
  click is tested against where each node was drawn. #449's "what a game
  writes" is this module's API.
- Input: a press lands on the topmost widget under the pointer, inside its
  clip, on nothing behind it; one focused widget a layer, Tab and
  Shift+Tab in the order made, the arrows, the d-pad and the stick to the
  nearest widget that way, both wrapping, Enter, Space and A to activate,
  Escape and B to close; a field takes typed characters as UTF-8 with
  Backspace, Delete, Home and End. A stack of menus: while one is open, or
  a field on a layer has the focus, the game's actions read nothing, and
  what was held stays swallowed until it is let go -- no shot from the
  click that pressed Resume.
- `ae3d.input` gathers the frame's events in order -- keys pressed and
  repeated, characters typed, mouse buttons pressed and released -- from
  GLFW's callbacks (new in `ae3d.platform`, with the window's content
  scale) and from injection (`inject_cursor`, `inject_char`,
  `inject_key_press`, `inject_scroll`); a tap or a click inside one frame
  now counts as down for the poll that takes it. `set_blocked` and
  `swallow_mouse` are what a menu blocks the game with. The agent
  channel's `input.set` takes a cursor, text and the wheel, and
  `input.get` says whether the game is blocked.
- `ae3d.hud`: clips (`push_clip`, `pop_clip`) that cut quads to a
  rectangle as they are made, exact and still one draw; images packed with
  a pixel of their own edge into one premultiplied RGBA atlas beside the
  glyphs', rewritten in place with only their rows sent to the device;
  `text_box` wrapping and aligning by the glyphs' ink (`glyphs.layout_box`,
  `measure_box`); a source drawn once a frame when the renderer draws the
  overlay; the frame's size. The overlay blends premultiplied. OpenGL and
  Vulkan (`ae3d.vkoverlay`, a second binding, per-slot staging, two
  timestamps a frame) draw the image atlas and time their own work.
- The editor (#460): its engine over the viewport has the overlay and the
  UI, cleared at the start of `engine_update` and drawn after the scene; a
  script reaches them through its object's scene
  (`behaviour.object_overlay`, `object_ui`), which `resources/scripts/
  health_bar.ae` draws a bar with. In play mode the viewport's pointer,
  buttons, keys and wheel are the game's input, so a game's menu works in
  the viewport as in the game. The editor checks it (`overlay_stuck`, in
  `ci.sh`), and its `idle_actions` check now counts a script that draws
  over the view as doing something.
- Measured (`tests/test_overlay_measure.ae`, both renderers, 1280 x 720):
  text at 96, 24 and 12 px stands 0.044, 0.062 and 0.106 px from the
  atlas's own glyph shapes on average (0.054, 0.060 and 0.106 from the
  font's outline), compared by pixels with a perfect area rendering of
  each, and is no softer than one (part-inked 0.91 of it at 96 px, where
  the same atlas as a scaled bitmap is 2.40 and 0.35 px off); 1,000
  rectangles cost 0.012 ms of CPU and 0.090 (OpenGL) / 0.022 ms (Vulkan)
  of the device's, 5,000 glyphs 0.16 ms and 0.093 / 0.076 ms; Vulkan
  against OpenGL, not a channel different. `tests/test_ui.ae`: nine
  anchors to the pixel at two sizes and three scales (36 of 36), 40 of 40
  clicks on the widget under them and none behind at two sizes and three
  scales, focus order 12 of 12 and arrows 6 of 6 with wrap-around, the pad
  and the stick stepping on time, a menu over a menu taking the input
  alone, a field
  ending "<Héllo Xwör!", the game hearing nothing in 22 frames of a menu,
  wrapped text and clipped panels with no pixel outside, images to the
  texel with no dark fringe, and the engine's pause menu clicked shut.
  `examples/game_hud.ae` is the HUD and a pause menu on the new layer;
  under the validation layer, synchronisation included, no error.
