### The Vulkan renderer in Aether, whole

- `native/gpu/vulkan.c` is gone (#398, #402), with the two headers the
  shader generator wrote for it (`vulkan_shaders.h`, `vulkan_uniforms.h`):
  5,671 lines of C, and 47,458 generated. What it still did moved
  to Aether on `contrib.vulkan.vk`, into four new modules and four that
  grew:
  - `ae3d.vkframe`, the frame: begun on its slot's fence, the scene pass
    opened by its first draw and split around the depth resolve, the
    cascades' and the lamps' shadow passes and the lamps' tile copies, the
    draws (direct, indirect for the device crowds, the sky, the screen
    passes), the post chain -- the reflection, the temporal pass or DLSS
    through the Streamline shim, the composite -- the stamps that split
    its GPU time, the hooks the meter, the overlay and the readback record
    through, the submit and the present;
  - `ae3d.vktargets`, what it draws into: the swapchain or the offscreen
    image, the scene's multisampled colour, depth and motion vectors, the
    passes over them, the cascades' 4,096-texel atlas, the lamps' atlas and
    its static layer, the camera depth, the reflection's and the temporal
    pass's colours, DLSS's output;
  - `ae3d.vkpipes`, the pipelines, from `ae3d.vkspirv`'s SPIR-V;
  - `ae3d.vkdesc`, the uniform blocks (one a program), the frame's ring of
    them, the descriptor sets and their cache, and the clustered lamps'
    storage buffers at bindings 6 and 7;
  - `ae3d.vkhost` gained images, samplers, the command pool, the one-off
    command buffers and the upload batch; `ae3d.vktexture` the table a
    draw binds textures from; `ae3d.vkmesh` the mesh table and the
    instance streams, packed over every core; `ae3d.vkdevice` the queues,
    the limits and the last error.
- The generator writes no C: a uniform's offset by its name is
  `vkscene.offset_of`, a binary search over the same sorted table the C
  header carried.
- A depth target sampled before its pass ran is taken through it first,
  as the C did for the cascades' atlas.
- Found on the way, and fixed: a string the device module kept as its last
  error was the local's that made it, freed as the function returned; the
  next error freed it again, and one run of `test_caustics` in three ended
  in a heap corruption (0xC0000374) where `core.model_free` let go of a
  string after it. Twelve runs clean since.
- Measured on an RTX 4070 Ti, the C frame core then the Aether one, back
  to back, three rounds each: `zombie_street` draws the same 88 draws,
  54,422 triangles, 5 program and 53 material binds (GPU: shadow
  0.091-0.104 ms against 0.097-0.103, scene 1.270-1.415 against
  1.336-1.432, post 0.196-0.241 against 0.234-0.240); `zombie_city` (400
  device-sorted figures in the rays, 1280x720) the same 305 draws,
  267,172 triangles, 6 and 217 binds, scene 6.480-6.625 ms against
  6.474-6.547, engine CPU work 1.240-1.349 against 1.269-1.326.
  `test_backend_parity` gives the same numbers channel for channel (21
  comparisons); the 59 Vulkan suites pass (`test_dlss` skips without the
  SDK); the Khronos layer with synchronization validation reports nothing
  on 22 of them, `test_lamp_shadows` (21 checks), `test_lamp_clusters`,
  `test_shadow_cascades`, `test_ray_shadows` and `test_engine_order`
  among them.
