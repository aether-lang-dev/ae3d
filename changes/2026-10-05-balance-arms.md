### Motion: the arms answer a shove

A POWERED figure shoved at 0.5 m/s or more now reacts with its arms (#606).
Below the startle (2.6 m/s), its arms used to hang on their clip, and a
staggering figure took its steps like a mannequin, arms straight at its
sides.

- **Graded.** The harder the shove, the higher and further forward the arms
  go. They lift 0.45 rad (doubling toward the startle) out to the sides.
  Toward the startle they swing forward, and at it they reach as before.
- **Forward of the body, whichever way the shove came.** Pushed onto its
  heels, a person throws the arms forward, against the fall. Arms swung the
  way the shove sent the body carried the weight with it, and the side and
  step-back figures in the showcase went over.
- **Timed like a person.** The arms move 0.15 s after the blow, rise over
  0.25 s, and settle over 0.35 s. They start from where the clip holds them
  under the chest as it is, and end back there. Aimed at once, the arms
  swung 9.7° in a frame. Lowered to hanging rather than to the clip, they
  swung again when the calm drawing came back in. The worst arm turn is now
  3.6° a frame (tests/test_calm_arms.ae).
- **Bracing** takes the arms from this reaction when a wall is in reach.

2.4 m/s at the wall, 1.2 from the side, 0.8 from in front, 1.0 and 2.9 from
