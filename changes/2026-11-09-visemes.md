### Visemes from a voice track (#910)

- **The mouth from the sound** (`ae3d.visemes.analyse`): a line's mono PCM
  read a frame every 10 ms through a 30 ms window, its loudness and, where
  voiced, its first two formants from a linear prediction
  (Levinson-Durbin, the envelope's first two peaks); each frame's five
  vowel visemes (A, I, U, E, O) by their nearness in log formant, the
  voice's own scale taken out first (Nearey's log-mean shift, held to the
  span from a large man's vocal tract to a child's), times how open the
  loudness says the mouth is; smoothed forward and back so a shape comes
  as its sound does. On a synthesised line at 48 kHz (a pulse train
  through formant resonators): every vowel frame told right, in a second
  voice with its formants 15% higher and its pitch at 210 Hz too; the
  open A's shape half on 10 ms before its sound and half off 20 ms after
  it stops (sound leading a face by more than about 45 ms is seen);
  2.8 s analysed in 4.5 ms.
- **Played as a layer of the animator's pose** (`animator.speak`): the
  visemes drive VRoid's vowel targets (`Fcl_MTH_A` to `O`) or ARKit's jaw
  and mouth shapes as the viseme-to-ARKit tables blend them
  (`visemes.drive`), the larger of the take's and the line's. On VRoid's
  base female the A target's weight is the track's within 3e-8.
- Not yet measured on recorded speech: no CC0 voice recording is in the
  fixtures, and consonants (closures, fricatives) are not told apart from
  silence and vowels.
