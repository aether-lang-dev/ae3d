### Aether 0.809.0

- **CI's AETHER_REF is v0.809.0**, the first release since 0.806.0 (0.807
  and 0.808 were tagged but never published). The motion, feet, controller
  and box man suites build on it with no warning and pass.

### Rising into a stance: play it before the rise

- **`motion.get_up` says what the way up ends in** (#761). It ends in the
  animation's pose as it stands when the rise begins. A game whose animator
  holds a strike while its fighter is down and plays the guard once the
  fighter is up cuts it from the strike's stance to the guard's at the
  hand-over, and it lurches. Over 24 get-ups of Siltreach's four fighters
  (knocked back and forward, rising into Idle or, after a Chop or a Thrust,
  into Idle_Sword), the lean a quarter second after the hand-over:

  | the guard played | mean | most |
  |---|---|---|
  | once up (the game) | 0.154 rad | 0.231 |
  | as the rise begins | 0.107 | 0.189 |
  | while it lies | 0.015 | 0.021 |

  No change to the engine: the stance a figure stands in after a cut is
  #767.
